NASA 罗曼太空望远镜升空，开启宇宙深空巡天

NASA's Roman Telescope lifts off, starting a mission to explore the universe's biggest mysteries
来源 Scientific American作者 Claire Cameron日期 2026-08-30难度 中等

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

1NASA's newest telescope is officially on its way to deep space after more than a decade of development and high anticipation. Onboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket, the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope lifted off from Launch Complex 39A at the Kennedy Space Center in Florida at 7:26 A.M. EDT on August 30.
2The telescope is set to transform our understanding of the cosmos—from the furthest reaches and most mysterious forces to the planets and stars that make up our own galaxy and even objects in our solar system.
3NASA administrator Jared Isaacman said at a postlaunch press conference that all systems appear nominal after launch. In the next weeks, the telescope's instruments will be calibrated and turned on as it travels to its point in space.
4The Roman Telescope will conduct its science observations from a perch at the second sun-Earth Lagrange point, or L2, a location some million miles "behind" our planet where the sun's and Earth's gravitational pulls balance each other out.
5"Roman is poised to conduct extraordinary surveys that will impact every area of astronomy," said Julie McEnery, Roman's senior project scientist, at a press conference ahead of the launch on Saturday.
6"We'll perform a revolutionary census of planets around other stars and may confirm that our standard model of the universe is incorrect and put us on the path to figuring out what is right," she added.
7With an initial science mission lasting five years, Roman will join NASA's Hubble Space Telescope and James Webb Space Telescope in revealing the universe in ways we have never seen before. Among the secrets Roman will probe is the nature of dark energy and dark matter.
8Roman is also outfitted with a special instrument called a coronagraph, which will enable the telescope to observe exoplanets with unprecedented sensitivity. "We think that we will be capable of detecting exoplanets that are about 100 million times fainter than their stars," says Vanessa Bailey, Roman coronagraph instrument scientist at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory.
9At the postlaunch press conference, Nicky Fox, associate administrator of NASA's Science Mission Directorate, said Roman could increase the number of known exoplanets from some 6,000 to 100,000.

四、全文大意

NASA 新一代太空望远镜"南希·格雷丝·罗曼"于 8 月 30 日由 SpaceX 猎鹰重型火箭从肯尼迪航天中心发射升空，前往距地球约一百万英里的第二拉格朗日点（L2）。这架历经十余年研制的望远镜将开展为期五年的科学任务，与哈勃、韦布望远镜并肩，重点探测暗能量与暗物质的本质，并对太阳系外行星进行"革命性普查"。其巡天图像规模惊人——单张图像相当于 50 多万台 4K 电视；搭载的日冕仪能以空前的灵敏度观测比恒星暗约一亿倍的系外行星。NASA 预计罗曼望远镜有望将已知系外行星数量从约 6000 颗提升至 10 万颗，首张图像预计明年初公布。

五、思考与仿写

思考题：报道提到罗曼望远镜可能"证实宇宙标准模型有误"。请结合第 6、7 段，谈谈为何"证伪现有理论"同样是科学仪器的一大价值，以及暗能量"是否恒定"这一问题为何如此关键。
仿写题：仿照第 8 段的 "… outfitted with a special instrument called a coronagraph, which will enable the telescope to observe … with unprecedented sensitivity."，用 "called … which will enable … to …" 结构描述一件工具或技术的功能。
