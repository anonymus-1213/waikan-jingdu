US Open: Carlos Alcaraz breezes past Tommy Paul in straight sets to advance to quarterfinal
来源 Yahoo Sports 雅虎体育（美）作者 未署名日期 2026-08-30难度 中等

中文导读

在 2026 年美网男单第四轮中，2 号种子、西班牙名将卡洛斯·阿尔卡拉斯直落三盘横扫美国选手汤米·保罗，强势晋级八强。这已是阿尔卡拉斯连续第 14 盘战胜保罗——后者自 2024 年温网以来再未从其手中拿下一盘。经历腕伤缺阵四个半月后，阿尔卡拉斯状态不减，在赢下前两盘的大满贯比赛中保持 63 胜 0 负的惊人战绩。本文记录了这场胜利，并展望了他冲击卫冕的八强之路。

【1】Carlos Alcaraz is moving on in the 2026 US Open after a definitive win over Tommy Paul, with the No. 2 ranked player beating the American in straight sets, 6-4, 6-3, 6-4.
【2】In a baffling stat, Sunday's three-set victory marks the 14th straight sets that Alcaraz has won against the American. Paul has not taken a set over Alcaraz since Wimbledon in 2024, even though the pair have faced off four times since then.
【3】Though Paul was unable to break through again this time, he did put up a fight in the first set: Alcaraz and Paul traded games until the young Spaniard finally broke the American's serve to eventually win 6-4.

The Spaniard kicked things off with a strong showing, beating Roman Safiullin in straight sets; though he dropped the first set to Jaime Faria in the second round, he pulled back for another win, and then beat Wu Yibing in straight sets as well.

But even after a long layoff, Alcaraz is continuing his dominance: He is now 63-0 in major tournaments after winning the first two sets.

【8】Paul, meanwhile, will fall out of a Grand Slam before the quarterfinal for the sixth consecutive tournament, with his last quarterfinal appearance coming at Roland-Garros in 2025.

四、全文大意

在 2026 年美网男单第四轮中，2 号种子、西班牙名将卡洛斯·阿尔卡拉斯直落三盘（6-4、6-3、6-4）横扫美国选手汤米·保罗，晋级八强。这已是阿尔卡拉斯连续第 14 盘战胜保罗，后者自 2024 年温网以来再未从其手中拿下一盘。经历腕伤缺阵四个半月后，阿尔卡拉斯状态未减，复出以来连克强敌，如今在赢下前两盘的大满贯比赛中保持 63 胜 0 负的惊人战绩。世界第一辛纳因伤退赛一度令男单签表显得开放，但阿尔卡拉斯依旧展现出统治力。他将在四分之一决赛对阵 8 号种子本·谢尔顿，力争卫冕去年美网冠军；而保罗则连续第六次在大满贯中止步八强之前。
