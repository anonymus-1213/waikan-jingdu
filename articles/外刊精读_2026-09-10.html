鸟鸣与森林漫步，何以成为减压良方

Birdsong and forest walks are tied to real mental-health benefits — but even light traffic noise can drown out the calm
来源 NC State University / University of Surrey / Mongabay（综合）作者 外刊精读专栏日期 2026-09-10难度 中等

中文导读

当你感到压力时，不妨听听鸟鸣、到林间走走。越来越多的研究发现，自然的声音与景色能带来可衡量的心理健康益处：北卡罗来纳州立大学的研究显示，鸟鸣与森林漫步和心理健康显著改善相关；英国萨里大学的一项研究则提醒，即便只是低强度的交通噪音，也会削弱鸟鸣的减压作用。在"修复过的城市森林"中静坐聆听鸟鸣的实验里，志愿者的压力下降、情绪改善，可一旦混入车流声，这种效果便大打折扣。对生活在噪音持续、绿地有限的城市里的人们——尤其是压力不断上升的年轻人——本文揭示了一条低成本、人人可用的减压之道。

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

【1】The sound of birds singing in a forest, a walk among trees, the sight of green leaves — these simple pleasures of nature are now backed by science. A growing body of research shows that the sights and sounds of nature can bring significant, measurable benefits to our mental health.
【2】A new study from North Carolina State University found that listening to birdsong and walking in forests were linked to significant improvements in mental health, compared with loud, urban environments. Researchers say nature's gentle sounds can help people feel calmer and more at ease.
【3】But not every green space works equally well. A separate study from the University of Surrey warns that even low-level traffic noise can weaken the power of birdsong to relieve stress. When the rumble of cars mixes with birdsong, the calming effect is much reduced.
【4】In one experiment, volunteers who sat in a restored urban forest while listening to birdsong reported lower stress and a better mood. The effect disappeared when the same scene was accompanied by the sound of passing traffic.
【5】This matters because most people now live in cities, where noise is constant and green spaces are limited. Stress and anxiety are on the rise, especially among young people, and nature offers a low-cost way to ease them.
【6】The findings point to practical solutions. Cities can protect quiet corners in parks, plant more trees along roads, and design spaces where the sounds of nature can still be heard. Such small changes could improve the mental health of millions of people.
【7】For ordinary people, the message is simple: when you feel stressed, try stepping outside, listening to the birds, and letting nature do its quiet work.

原文出处：本文根据北卡罗来纳州立大学（NC State，2026-07-21）、英国萨里大学（University of Surrey，2026-08-26）相关研究，及 Mongabay 报道（2026-07-27）综合整理编写，供学习使用。

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

The sound of birds singing in a forest, a walk among trees, the sight of green leaves — these simple pleasures of nature are now backed by science.
主干：these simple pleasures of nature are now backed by science（主语 + 被动语态 be backed by）
列举性同位语：The sound of birds singing in a forest, a walk among trees, the sight of green leaves（三个并列名词短语，破折号后总结）
后置定语（现在分词短语）：singing in a forest（修饰 birds）
考点：现在分词短语作后置定语；be backed by 被动语态；破折号引出总结性主语
译文：森林里鸟儿的鸣唱、林间的漫步、绿叶的景致——这些简单的大自然乐趣如今都有了科学依据。
A new study from North Carolina State University found that listening to birdsong and walking in forests were linked to significant improvements in mental health, compared with loud, urban environments.
主干：A new study found that …（主语 + found + that 宾语从句）
宾语从句：listening to birdsong and walking in forests were linked to significant improvements（动名词短语并列作主语，谓语用复数 were）
状语：compared with loud, urban environments（过去分词短语作状语，意为"与……相比"）
考点：动名词短语并列作主语；be linked to 搭配；compared with 过去分词作状语
译文：北卡罗来纳州立大学的一项新研究发现，与嘈杂的城市环境相比，聆听鸟鸣和在森林中散步与心理健康的显著改善相关。
When the rumble of cars mixes with birdsong, the calming effect is much reduced.
主干：the calming effect is much reduced（主语 + 被动语态）
时间状语从句：When the rumble of cars mixes with birdsong（"当……时"）
考点：when 引导时间状语从句；mix with 搭配；被动语态 is reduced
译文：当汽车的隆隆声与鸟鸣混杂在一起时，这种镇静效果便大打折扣。
In one experiment, volunteers who sat in a restored urban forest while listening to birdsong reported lower stress and a better mood.
主干：volunteers reported lower stress and a better mood（主语 + reported + 并列宾语）
定语从句：who sat in a restored urban forest（修饰 volunteers）
时间状语（省略结构）：while listening to birdsong（while + 现在分词，省略 they were）
考点：who 引导定语从句；while doing 省略主语与 be 动词
译文：在一项实验中，坐在修复过的城市森林里聆听鸟鸣的志愿者报告了更低的压力和更好的情绪。
This matters because most people now live in cities, where noise is constant and green spaces are limited.
主干：This matters（主系表结构）
原因状语从句：because most people now live in cities
非限制性定语从句：where noise is constant and green spaces are limited（where 指代 cities）
考点：because 引导原因状语从句；where 引导非限制性定语从句（先行词为地点名词）
译文：这一点很重要，因为如今大多数人生活在城市里，那里噪音持续不断，绿地却十分有限。

四、全文大意

鸟儿的鸣唱、林间的漫步，这些简单的大自然乐趣正获得越来越多科学证据的支持。北卡罗来纳州立大学的研究发现，与嘈杂的城市环境相比，聆听鸟鸣和在森林中散步与心理健康的显著改善相关；英国萨里大学的研究则提醒，即便是低强度的交通噪音，也会削弱鸟鸣缓解压力的作用。在一项实验中，坐在修复过的城市森林里聆听鸟鸣的志愿者报告了更低的压力和更好的情绪，而一旦混入往来车辆的声音，这种效果便几乎消失。这之所以重要，是因为如今大多数人生活在噪音持续、绿地有限的城市里，压力与焦虑——尤其是年轻人的——正不断上升，而亲近自然提供了一种低成本的缓解方式。研究给出了切实可行的方向：保护公园里的安静角落、沿路多种树、设计出能听见自然之声的城市空间，这些小小的改变或许就能改善千百万人的心理健康。对普通人而言，启示很简单：感到压力时，不妨走出户外，聆听鸟鸣，让大自然安静地发挥它的作用。
