深睡眠里的"清洁工"：科学家揭示大脑如何趁你熟睡"冲洗"废物

A study reveals how slow, rhythmic waves of a signalling molecule pump cerebrospinal fluid through the sleeping brain to flush out metabolic waste
来源 Cell / University of Copenhagen / Nature News（综合）作者 外刊精读专栏日期 2026-09-23难度 难

中文导读

我们常听说"睡眠是大脑的排毒时间"，但大脑究竟如何"自我清洁"，长期是个谜。十年前，科学家发现大脑有一套名为"类淋巴系统"的废物清除网络——脑脊液像潮水一样流经脑组织，把代谢废物冲刷出去。2025 年发表在《细胞》的一项研究，首次看清了驱动这股"潮水"的"泵"：深睡眠时，脑干会以大约每 50 秒一次的节奏释放去甲肾上腺素，让血管有规律地收缩，像泵一样把脑脊液压进大脑深处、洗走包括与阿尔茨海默病相关的蛋白在内的废物。更令人警醒的是，研究者在实验中还发现，常用安眠药唑吡坦会削弱这种"泵血"节律。本文带你走近这场大脑深处的"夜间大扫除"。

【1】Every night, while we sleep, the brain appears to give itself a thorough cleaning, yet for decades scientists could not explain precisely how. The discovery of the glymphatic system in 2013 showed that cerebrospinal fluid flows through the brain's tissue, but what drives this tide has remained one of neuroscience's most stubborn puzzles.
【2】A study published in the journal Cell, led by researchers at the University of Copenhagen and the University of Oxford, now claims to have located the long-sought "pump". The team focused on norepinephrine, a signalling molecule that the brainstem releases in slow, rhythmic waves during deep, non-REM sleep.
【3】The researchers observed that every fifty seconds or so, a surge of norepinephrine makes blood vessels in the brain contract, and each contraction squeezes cerebrospinal fluid through the tissue, flushing out metabolic waste. This slow "vasomotion", they argue, acts as a piston whose regular strokes wash the sleeping brain.
【4】To watch the process unfold, the scientists implanted fibre-optic probes into the brains of living mice, recording norepinephrine levels and fluid movement in real time as the animals slept. With fluorescent tracers injected into the fluid, the team could track the passage of waste from deep regions toward the surface.
【5】The waste being removed includes amyloid-beta, the sticky protein whose accumulation is a hallmark of Alzheimer's disease. Sleep has long been linked to the risk of dementia, and the new findings, if they hold, provide a mechanical explanation for that connection.
【6】Most strikingly, the team tested zolpidem, a widely used sleep medication, and found that it suppressed the norepinephrine waves and reduced fluid transport. This raises the unsettling possibility that some sleep aids, by sedating the brain, may impair the very clearance that sleep exists to perform.
【7】The authors are careful to stress that the experiments were performed in mice, and that the results cannot yet be translated directly to humans. Whether the pump can be strengthened to prevent dementia — let alone to treat it — remains an open question demanding years of clinical study.
【8】Independent researchers praised the work for closing a critical gap, while cautioning that a good night's sleep is shaped by many factors, from stress to caffeine. Rarely, they note, has a single experiment made so vividly clear that sleep is not a passive state but an active, physical process of self-repair.
【9】For the public, the message is at once humbling and practical. The body's nightly maintenance, driven by rhythms we barely notice, reminds us that sleep is not an indulgence to be sacrificed, but a physiological necessity whose benefits we are only beginning to understand.

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

The discovery of the glymphatic system in 2013 showed that cerebrospinal fluid flows through the brain's tissue, but what drives this tide has remained one of neuroscience's most stubborn puzzles.
主干：The discovery ... showed that ... , but what drives this tide has remained ...（并列句：showed + that 宾语从句；but 转折连接第二分句）
宾语从句：that cerebrospinal fluid flows through the brain's tissue（作 showed 的宾语）
主语从句：what drives this tide（what 引导，作第二分句的主语）
考点：that 引导宾语从句；what 引导主语从句；并列连词 but 连接的并列句
译文：2013 年"类淋巴系统"的发现表明，脑脊液流经大脑组织，但究竟是什么驱动了这股"潮水"，一直是神经科学最棘手的难题之一。
A study published in the journal Cell, led by researchers at the University of Copenhagen and the University of Oxford, now claims to have located the long-sought "pump".
主干：A study ... now claims to have located the long-sought "pump"（主语 + claims + 不定式短语）
过去分词作后置定语：published in the journal Cell（修饰 study）
过去分词作后置定语：led by researchers at the University of Copenhagen and the University of Oxford（修饰 study）
考点：过去分词短语作后置定语（语法填空常考 done 的选择）；claim to do / to have done 不定式完成式
译文：一项发表于《细胞》杂志、由哥本哈根大学和牛津大学研究人员领导的研究，如今声称已找到了那个寻觅已久的"泵"。
The researchers observed that every fifty seconds or so, a surge of norepinephrine makes blood vessels in the brain contract, and each contraction squeezes cerebrospinal fluid through the tissue, flushing out metabolic waste.
主干：The researchers observed that ...（主语 + observed + that 宾语从句）
宾语从句（并列）：a surge of norepinephrine makes blood vessels ... contract, and each contraction squeezes cerebrospinal fluid ...
使役结构：makes blood vessels contract（make + 宾语 + 动词原形作宾补）
现在分词作结果状语：flushing out metabolic waste（"从而冲走代谢废物"）
考点：make + 宾语 + 宾补（省略 to 的不定式）；现在分词作结果状语
译文：研究人员观察到，大约每五十秒，一股去甲肾上腺素就会令大脑中的血管收缩，而每一次收缩都挤压脑脊液穿过组织，从而冲走代谢废物。
This raises the unsettling possibility that some sleep aids, by sedating the brain, may impair the very clearance that sleep exists to perform.
主干：This raises the unsettling possibility（主语 + raises + 宾语）
同位语从句：that some sleep aids ... may impair the very clearance（解释 possibility 的内容）
插入的介词短语（方式状语）：by sedating the brain（"通过使大脑镇静"）
定语从句：that sleep exists to perform（修饰 clearance，"睡眠存在就是为了完成的清除"）
考点：that 引导同位语从句；by doing 作方式状语；that 引导定语从句；the very + 名词（表强调）
译文：这引出一个令人不安的可能性：某些助眠药物通过使大脑镇静，可能恰恰损害了睡眠存在本身所要完成的清除作用。
The body's nightly maintenance, driven by rhythms we barely notice, reminds us that sleep is not an indulgence to be sacrificed, but a physiological necessity whose benefits we are only beginning to understand.
主干：The body's nightly maintenance ... reminds us that ...（主语 + reminds + 宾语 + that 宾语从句）
过去分词作后置定语：driven by rhythms we barely notice（修饰 maintenance；内含省略关系词的定语从句 we barely notice）
宾语从句：that sleep is not an indulgence ... but a physiological necessity（not ... but ... 结构）
定语从句：whose benefits we are only beginning to understand（whose 引导，修饰 necessity）
考点：过去分词作定语；not ... but ...；whose 引导定语从句
译文：身体夜间的自我维护，由我们几乎察觉不到的节律所驱动，提醒我们：睡眠不是一种可以牺牲的享受，而是一种生理必需——其益处我们才刚刚开始理解。

四、全文大意

睡眠时大脑会"自我清洗"，但驱动这一过程的具体机制长期是神经科学的难题。2013 年科学家发现了"类淋巴系统"——脑脊液流经脑组织、冲走代谢废物，却不知这股"潮水"由何驱动。2025 年发表在《细胞》的一项研究，由哥本哈根大学与牛津大学团队完成，终于找到了那个"泵"：深睡眠（非快速眼动睡眠）期间，脑干会以大约每 50 秒一次的节奏释放神经递质去甲肾上腺素，令大脑血管有规律地收缩，而每一次收缩都像活塞一样挤压脑脊液穿过脑组织，把废物冲刷出去。研究者在活体小鼠脑中植入光纤探针，并用荧光示踪剂实时追踪废物流向，观察到了这一过程。被冲走的废物中包含淀粉样蛋白——这种黏性蛋白的堆积正是阿尔茨海默病的标志，而睡眠与痴呆风险的联系由此获得了一个机理层面的解释。最令人警醒的是，团队测试了常用安眠药唑吡坦，发现它会抑制去甲肾上腺素的节律性释放、削弱脑脊液的流动，这提示某些助眠药可能反而损害了睡眠的清洁功能。不过，作者谨慎强调实验基于小鼠、尚不能直接外推到人，能否通过强化这个"泵"来预防乃至治疗痴呆，仍是有待多年临床研究的开放问题。独立研究者赞赏该研究填补了关键空白，同时提醒良好睡眠受压力、咖啡因等诸多因素影响。这项研究最鲜明地说明：睡眠并非被动静止，而是一个主动的、生理性的自我修复过程；身体的夜间维护提醒我们，睡眠是不可牺牲的生理必需。
