罗伯·莱纳逝世九个月后，追授艾美奖最佳客串男演员

Rob Reiner Wins Posthumous Emmy for 'The Bear'
来源 Variety 综艺（美）作者 Clayton Davis（首席颁奖编辑）日期 2026-08-31难度 中等

中文导读

美国著名导演、演员罗伯·莱纳在逝世九个月后，凭借《熊家餐馆》荣获艾美奖喜剧类最佳客串男演员。这是对去年 12 月与妻子一同遇难的他的追授荣誉，也是自 1995 年以来首个追授给银幕表演的艾美奖。颁奖人代为领奖时，全场起立鼓掌长达一分钟。此次获奖还打破了其父卡尔·莱纳保持的演艺类艾美奖最长获奖间隔纪录：罗伯·莱纳距 1978 年上次获奖相隔 48 年，超过父亲此前保持的 37 年。

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

【1】The late and great Rob Reiner has won an Emmy, nine months after his death.
【2】Reiner won the outstanding guest actor in a comedy series for "The Bear" at the Emmy Awards Night 2, prevailing over Michael J. Fox and Brett Goldstein ("Shrinking"), Hamish Linklater ("Widow's Bay"), Christopher McDonald ("Hacks") and Connor Storrie ("Saturday Night Live"). It is a posthumous honor for the director and actor who was killed alongside his wife, Michele Singer Reiner, in December — and the first posthumous Emmy for an on-camera performance since Raúl Julia won for "The Burning Season" in 1995.
【3】Presenter Wendi McLendon-Covey accepted the Emmy on Reiner's behalf to a minute-long standing ovation from the audience.
【4】The win also sets a record and rewrites one that his own father, Carl Reiner, set. Rob Reiner had not won an Emmy since 1978, when he took his second consecutive supporting actor trophy for the classic comedy "All in the Family." The 48-year gap between that win and this newest one shatters the mark for the longest span between acting Emmy wins — 37 years — held by Carl Reiner, who went from his 1958 win for "Caesar's Hour" to a 1995 guest comedy Emmy for "Mad About You." The elder Reiner set the record in guest comedy and his son now breaks it in the same category.
【5】Reiner, who died at 78, has been remembered across the industry as the filmmaker behind classics such as "This Is Spinal Tap," "The Princess Bride," "When Harry Met Sally" and "A Few Good Men," and was honored in the In Memoriam segment at the 2026 Oscars. His guest turn on FX's "The Bear" stands as one of his final screen performances.
【6】This year's Emmy nominations were led by "The Pitt" with 25 nominations, followed by "Hacks," "Widow's Bay" and "Beef." An edited version of the first two nights of the Emmys will air on FXX on Saturday, Sept. 12, at 8 p.m. ET, while the live 78th Emmys broadcast on NBC takes place on Monday, Sept. 14, at 8 p.m. ET.
