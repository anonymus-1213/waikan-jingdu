为乐趣而读书的孩子，正在越来越少

A growing body of evidence suggests that reading for pleasure is in steep decline among the young — a shift researchers say could quietly reshape empathy, attention and academic achievement
来源 The Guardian / National Literacy Trust / NAEP（综合）作者 外刊精读专栏日期 2026-09-11难度 较高

📌 本文综合美国国家教育进展评估（NAEP）、英国国家读写信托（National Literacy Trust）等机构的公开报告与调查数据改编编写，语言难度已对标高考阅读理解真题（CET6 / 超纲词密集、句式复杂）。

中文导读

长辈总说"读书能打开世界的大门"，但越来越多证据表明：愿意为乐趣而阅读的年轻人正在急剧减少，而这一转变的影响远比大多数家长和教育者意识到的更为深远。美国国家教育进展评估显示，2023 年每天为乐趣读书的 13 岁学生占比已从 2012 年的 27% 骤降至 14%；英国国家读写信托的全国调查同样发现，空闲时间每日阅读的儿童比例从 2005 年的 38% 跌至仅五分之一。男孩的退步尤为明显，且随年龄增长差距不断拉大。本文剖析这一趋势的数据、影响、成因与破解之道，并指出：在充斥数字干扰的时代，静心读完一个故事，或许正是我们能送给下一代最珍贵的礼物。

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

【1】For generations, adults have assured children that books are windows to the world. Yet a mounting body of evidence now suggests that the habit of reading for pleasure is in steep decline among the young — a shift that researchers argue carries consequences far more profound than many parents and educators realise.
【2】The statistics are sobering. According to the United States' National Assessment of Educational Progress, only 14 percent of 13-year-olds reported reading for enjoyment almost every day in 2023, a precipitous drop from 27 percent in 2012. Britain tells a strikingly similar story: a nationwide survey by the National Literacy Trust found that merely one in five children and young people now reads daily in their spare time, down from 38 percent in 2005.
【3】The retreat is especially pronounced among boys, and the gap widens with age. By the time they reach 14 to 16, only about one in five boys says he enjoys reading, compared with roughly two in five girls. Having abandoned books as they entered adolescence, many boys have redirected their attention toward video games, social media feeds and short-form videos, which deliver instant gratification but rarely require sustained concentration.
【4】Why should this matter? Because reading for pleasure is far more than a leisure pursuit. Research consistently shows that children who read for enjoyment tend to outperform their peers academically, acquire a richer vocabulary and develop the capacity to think critically and at length.
【5】Perhaps most important, reading fiction in particular enables young people to inhabit the minds of others, cultivating the quality scientists call empathy — the ability to understand and share another person's feelings. When children cease to read, they also forfeit a rare opportunity to practise sustained attention, a cognitive skill that short videos, by their very design, seldom reward.
【6】The underlying causes are not difficult to identify. The ubiquitous presence of smartphones has transformed idle moments — once filled by books — into opportunities for scrolling. Compounding this, leisure reading competes for children's time against a widening array of digital distractions, while the pressure of academic testing leaves ever less room for the unhurried pleasure of a good novel.
【7】Nevertheless, the decline is not irreversible. Experts maintain that children are far more likely to embrace books when they are allowed to choose what they read, when their parents model reading beside them, and when schools deliberately safeguard quiet time for free reading. Even modest interventions — a well-stocked classroom library, a ten-minute daily reading ritual — can, over time, rekindle a lifelong love of literature.
【8】In an age saturated with endless distraction, the ability to sit still with a single story may prove to be one of the most valuable gifts we can bestow upon the next generation — a quiet act of resistance against the fragmenting pull of the screen.

段落主旨

  1. 为乐趣阅读的习惯在年轻人中急剧衰退，影响比想象更深远。
  2. 美英数据：每日为乐趣阅读的比例十余年间大幅下滑。
  3. 男孩退步尤其明显，进入青春期后转向游戏与短视频。
  4. 为何重要：为乐趣阅读能提升学业、词汇量与深度思考。
  5. 读小说培养同理心；不阅读则丧失长时间专注的机会。
  6. 成因：智能手机侵占闲暇，短视频与考试压力挤压阅读。
  7. 但可逆转：自主选书、家长示范、学校留时间能重燃阅读。
  8. 在充斥干扰的时代，静心读完一个故事是珍贵的礼物。

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

Yet a mounting body of evidence now suggests that the habit of reading for pleasure is in steep decline among the young — a shift that researchers argue carries consequences far more profound than many parents and educators realise.
主干：a mounting body of evidence … suggests that …（主语 + suggests + that 宾语从句）
宾语从句：the habit of reading for pleasure is in steep decline among the young
同位语 + 定语从句：a shift that researchers argue carries consequences …（破折号引出，内含插入语 researchers argue，that 在从句中作主语）
考点：that 引导宾语从句与定语从句的区分；名词短语作同位语；far more … than 比较结构
译文：然而，越来越多的证据表明，为乐趣而阅读的习惯在年轻人中正急剧衰退——研究人员认为，这一转变带来的后果远比许多家长和教育者意识到的更为深远。
According to the United States' National Assessment of Educational Progress, only 14 percent of 13-year-olds reported reading for enjoyment almost every day in 2023, a precipitous drop from 27 percent in 2012.
主干：only 14 percent of 13-year-olds reported reading for enjoyment（主语 + reported + 动名词短语）
方式状语：According to the United States' National Assessment of Educational Progress（"根据……"）
同位语：a precipitous drop from 27 percent in 2012（对前句内容的补充说明）
考点：report doing sth 结构（语法填空常考动名词作宾语）；名词短语作同位语
译文：根据美国国家教育进展评估，2023 年只有 14% 的 13 岁学生表示几乎每天都为乐趣而阅读，这一比例较 2012 年的 27% 急剧下降。
Having abandoned books as they entered adolescence, many boys have redirected their attention toward video games, social media feeds and short-form videos, which deliver instant gratification but rarely require sustained concentration.
主干：many boys have redirected their attention toward video games … and short-form videos（主语 + have redirected + 宾语）
现在分词完成式作状语：Having abandoned books as they entered adolescence（内含 as 引导的时间状语从句）
非限制性定语从句：which deliver instant gratification but rarely require sustained concentration（which 指代前面并列的宾语）
考点：现在分词完成式 having done（表示先于谓语发生）；which 引导非限制性定语从句
译文：许多男孩在进入青春期时就放下了书本，转而把注意力投向了电子游戏、社交媒体动态和短视频——这些东西能带来即时的满足感，却很少需要持续的专注。
When children cease to read, they also forfeit a rare opportunity to practise sustained attention, a cognitive skill that short videos, by their very design, seldom reward.
主干：they also forfeit a rare opportunity（主语 + forfeit + 宾语）
时间状语从句：When children cease to read（"当……时"）
后置定语（不定式）：to practise sustained attention（修饰 opportunity）
同位语 + 定语从句：a cognitive skill that short videos … seldom reward（内含插入语 by their very design）
考点：when 引导时间状语从句；不定式作后置定语；that 引导定语从句（作主语）；插入语
译文：当孩子们不再阅读时，他们也就丧失了一个练习"持续专注"的宝贵机会——而持续专注恰恰是短视频因其设计本身很少给予回报的认知能力。
Experts maintain that children are far more likely to embrace books when they are allowed to choose what they read, when their parents model reading beside them, and when schools deliberately safeguard quiet time for free reading.
主干：Experts maintain that …（主语 + maintain + that 宾语从句）
宾语从句：children are far more likely to embrace books（be likely to do 结构）
三个并列的时间状语从句：when they are allowed to choose … / when their parents model … / when schools deliberately safeguard …
考点：be likely to do sth；并列 when 状语从句；what 引导宾语从句（choose what they read）
译文：专家们认为，当孩子被允许自主选择读什么、当父母在一旁示范阅读、当学校有意识地保障自由阅读的安静时间时，孩子们就更可能欣然拿起书本。

四、全文大意

长辈常言"读书打开世界的大门"，但越来越多证据表明，为乐趣而阅读的习惯正在年轻人中急剧衰退，且其影响远比表面看起来深远。美国国家教育进展评估显示，2023 年每天为乐趣阅读的 13 岁学生占比已从 2012 年的 27% 骤降至 14%；英国国家读写信托的全国调查也发现，空闲时间每日阅读的儿童比例从 2005 年的 38% 跌至仅五分之一。男孩的退步尤为明显、且随年龄拉大——进入青春期后，他们纷纷放下书本，转向能带来即时满足却无需持续专注的电子游戏与短视频。这一趋势为何重要？因为为乐趣阅读远非消遣：它让孩子学业更出色、词汇更丰富、思考更深入；读小说尤其能让孩子"走进他人内心"，培养同理心，而不阅读则使孩子丧失练习持续专注的宝贵机会。究其成因，智能手机无处不在，把原本属于书本的闲暇时光变成了刷屏时间，叠加数字干扰的增多与应试压力对阅读空间的挤压。所幸这种衰退并非不可逆转——让孩子自主选书、家长示范阅读、学校保障自由阅读时间，都能重燃对文学的终身热爱。在这个充斥干扰的时代，静下心来读完一个故事，或许正是我们能赠予下一代最珍贵的礼物，也是对屏幕碎片化牵引的一种安静抵抗。

五、思考与仿写

思考题：结合第 2、3 段的数据，分析"男孩阅读退步更明显、且随年龄增长差距不断拉大"背后可能的原因；并结合你的观察，谈谈智能手机与短视频如何重塑了青少年的闲暇方式。
仿写题：仿照第 5 段 "When children cease to read, they also forfeit a rare opportunity to practise sustained attention" 中「When + 从句, 主语 + forfeit/lose + 名词短语 + 不定式后置定语」的句式，围绕「坚持运动 / 远离屏幕」写一个句子。
