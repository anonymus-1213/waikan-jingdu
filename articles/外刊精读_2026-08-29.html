英国特殊教育需求计划申请激增，占比或将翻倍至十分之一

Pupils with Send plans in England likely to double to one in 10 in rush for support
来源 The Guardian 卫报作者 卫报教育版日期 2026-08-29难度 中等

中文导读

英格兰的特殊教育正面临一场申请潮。由于政府即将收紧特殊教育需求（EHCP）计划的发放，许多家庭纷纷抢在改革落地前为孩子申请支持，学校和议会预计拥有 EHCP 的儿童比例将从二十分之一翻倍至十分之一。这一激增超出政府预测，蚕食改善主流学校支持的资金，并给地方财政带来沉重压力。本文带你了解 EHCP 是什么、申请为何激增，以及这场政策"预告效应"引发的连锁反应。

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

【1】Schools and councils across England are expecting the proportion of children with special needs plans to double to one in 10, with families rushing to secure support before ministers bring in changes.
【2】Headteachers have reported an "unreal" surge in applications for education, health and care plans (EHCPs) mandating support for needs or disabilities such as speech and learning difficulties, autism or ADHD. In 2025, just one in 20 school-age children had EHCPs.
【3】The number of pupils with EHCPs has risen by more than 11% in each of the past two years, and projections by local authorities seen by the Guardian are anticipating similar double-digit annual increases over the next three years.
【4】Such a rapid increase would outstrip the government's own forecasts and eat into funding for improving specialist support in mainstream schools, undermining its plans for immediate reform while straining school and council budgets.
【5】EHCPs are legal documents agreed between local authorities and families in England detailing the support required for children and young people up to the age of 25, allowing access to extra funding or places in special schools. While more than 530,000 school-age children have EHCPs, an additional 1.3 million have identified special needs, according to official data.
【6】The costs are met out of local authority high needs budgets but the massive growth in EHCPs has led to councils amassing sustained deficits, and require a statutory override granted by central government to avoid many local authorities becoming technically bankrupt.
【7】Paul Whiteman, the general secretary of the National Association of Head Teachers, said: "While it is hard to give a definitive reason for why this is happening, it appears likely that the government's plans to restrict future EHCPs to children with the most complex needs may have led to more families and schools applying before these changes kick in."
【8】The surge in applications has led some local authorities to dramatically raise their own forecasts. A report by Hampshire council in July showed applications so far this year were at record levels and forecasted a 58% increase in EHCPs between 2026 and 2030. Manchester council is also anticipating a 40% increase in school-age children with EHCPs by 2029.

原文出处：The Guardian（卫报教育版，2026-09-06）。报道对象为英格兰特殊教育需求（Send）EHCP 申请激增及其对地方财政的影响。

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

Schools and councils across England are expecting the proportion of children with special needs plans to double to one in 10, with families rushing to secure support before ministers bring in changes.
主干：Schools and councils across England are expecting（英格兰各地的学校和议会预计）
宾语：the proportion of children with special needs plans to double to one in 10（有特殊需求计划的儿童比例将翻倍至十分之一；with special needs plans 修饰 children）
独立主格结构：with families rushing to secure support（with + 名词 + 现在分词，表伴随，各家庭争相获得支持）
时间状语从句：before ministers bring in changes（在部长们推行改革之前）
译文：英格兰各地的学校和议会预计，拥有特殊需求计划的儿童比例将翻倍至十分之一，因为各家庭纷纷在部长们推行改革之前争相为孩子争取支持。
Such a rapid increase would outstrip the government's own forecasts and eat into funding for improving specialist support in mainstream schools, undermining its plans for immediate reform while straining school and council budgets.
主干：Such a rapid increase would outstrip the government's own forecasts and eat into funding（如此快速的增长将超过政府自身的预测，并蚕食资金）
目的/用途修饰：for improving specialist support in mainstream schools（用于改善主流学校的专业支持）
结果状语（分词）：undermining its plans for immediate reform（削弱其立即改革的计划）
并列伴随：while straining school and council budgets（同时使学校和议会的预算承压）
译文：如此快速的增长将超出政府自身的预测，并蚕食用于改善主流学校专业支持的资金，削弱其立即改革的计划，同时给学校和议会的预算带来压力。
EHCPs are legal documents agreed between local authorities and families in England detailing the support required for children and young people up to the age of 25, allowing access to extra funding or places in special schools.
主干：EHCPs are legal documents（EHCP 是法定文件）
过去分词定语：agreed between local authorities and families in England（由英格兰地方政府与家庭双方商定）
现在分词定语：detailing the support required（详述所需支持）
结果状语（分词）：allowing access to extra funding or places in special schools（从而可获得额外资金或特殊学校名额）
译文：EHCP 是由英格兰地方政府与家庭双方商定的法定文件，详述了 25 岁以下儿童和青少年所需的支持，使其能够获得额外资金或特殊学校名额。
"While it is hard to give a definitive reason for why this is happening, it appears likely that the government's plans to restrict future EHCPs to children with the most complex needs may have led to more families and schools applying before these changes kick in."
让步状语从句：While it is hard to give a definitive reason for why this is happening（尽管很难为这种情况给出确切原因）
主干：it appears likely（it 为形式主语，似乎很可能）
主语从句：that the government's plans … may have led to more families and schools applying（政府的计划……可能导致更多家庭和学校提出申请）
后置定语：to restrict future EHCPs to children with the most complex needs（将未来 EHCP 限定于需求最复杂的儿童）
时间状语从句：before these changes kick in（在这些改革生效之前）
译文："尽管很难为这一现象给出确切原因，但很可能是因为政府计划将未来的 EHCP 限定给需求最复杂的儿童，导致更多家庭和学校在这些改革生效之前抢先提出申请。"

四、全文大意

英格兰各地的学校和议会预计，拥有教育、健康和照护计划（EHCP）的儿童比例将翻倍，从 2025 年的二十分之一升至十分之一。由于政府宣布从 2 月起改革特殊教育体系，各家庭纷纷在改革落地前为孩子抢申 EHCP，申请量出现"不真实"的激增，过去两年每年增幅都超过 11%。这一激增将超出政府预测，蚕食改善主流学校支持的资金，并给学校和议会带来沉重财政压力。目前全英已有超过 53 万学龄儿童持有 EHCP，另有 130 万儿童被认定有特殊需求；地方议会预测到 2028-29 年 EHCP 数量将达 84 万。教育大臣 Georgia Gould 承诺建立更包容的体系，让家长"不必再为支持而抗争"。
