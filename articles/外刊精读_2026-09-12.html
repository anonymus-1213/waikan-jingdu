印度洋海底山脉发现 149 个新物种，深海生命远超想象

An expedition to unexplored seamounts near Australia's Christmas and Cocos islands has uncovered 149 species new to science, revealing how little we still know about the deep ocean
来源 Mongabay / Australian Museum / The Cool Down（综合）作者 外刊精读专栏日期 2026-09-12难度 较高

中文导读

地球上海洋覆盖了超过三分之二的表面，但深海至今仍是人类探索最少的"最后疆域"。一支国际考察队把目标对准了澳大利亚圣诞岛与科科斯（基林）群岛附近、远离任何大陆的一片海底山脉，结果一举带回 149 个科学界从未描述过的新物种——包括一种梦参属海参、一种青眼鱼科的鱼，以及一种寄生于其他深海动物的生物。这些海底山像散布在荒芜深海平原上的"生命孤岛"，在数百万年的隔绝中演化出独一无二的物种，却也因底拖网捕捞与海底采矿而岌岌可危。本文带我们潜入这片未被扰动的水下世界，思考"在消失之前先认识它们"的紧迫与意义。

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

【1】The deep ocean, which covers more than two-thirds of our planet, remains one of the least explored frontiers on Earth. A single expedition, conducted near two remote Australian islands, has just shown how much life there is still waiting to be found — 149 species that science had never described before.
【2】Led by marine biologists from Australia's leading research institutions, the voyage targeted a chain of seamounts — ancient underwater volcanoes — rising from the floor of the Indian Ocean near Christmas Island and the Cocos (Keeling) Islands, a territory that lies far from any continent.
【3】What the team brought back astonished even seasoned researchers: among the 149 new species were a sea cucumber belonging to the genus Oneirophanta, a greeneye fish from the family Chlorophthalmus, and a parasitic creature that attaches itself to other deep-sea animals.
【4】To reach depths where sunlight never penetrates, the scientists relied on remotely operated vehicles and baited cameras, which descended thousands of metres to film and collect specimens from habitats that had never before been disturbed by human hands.
【5】Seamounts, the researchers explain, act as islands of life in an otherwise barren abyssal plain. Isolated for millions of years, their slopes and summits have allowed countless creatures to evolve in ways found nowhere else on Earth.
【6】Yet the very isolation that made this diversity possible has also left it dangerously exposed. Deep-sea bottom trawling and seabed mining, both of which are expanding as resources on land grow scarce, could destroy these fragile ecosystems long before most of their inhabitants have even been named.
【7】"Naming a new species is not a quick process," one researcher noted, adding that many of the 149 creatures may take years of careful study to describe formally. This, the team argues, makes protecting such places urgent rather than optional.
【8】The discovery, published in a series of scientific papers, forms part of a broader global effort to catalogue the ocean's biodiversity before it disappears. Scientists estimate that as many as two million marine species may exist, the majority of which remain unknown.
【9】For the public, the message is at once humbling and hopeful. The fact that a single voyage can uncover so much suggests that the ocean, though under growing pressure, still holds wonders capable of inspiring the next generation of explorers and conservationists alike.

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

A single expedition, conducted near two remote Australian islands, has just shown how much life there is still waiting to be found — 149 species that science had never described before.
主干：A single expedition ... has just shown ...（主语 + has shown + 宾语从句）
后置定语（过去分词短语）：conducted near two remote Australian islands（修饰 expedition）
宾语从句：how much life there is still waiting to be found（how much 引导，作 shown 的宾语）
破折号同位语 + 定语从句：149 species that science had never described before（that 引导定语从句修饰 species，时态为过去完成时）
考点：过去分词作后置定语；how much 引导宾语从句；that 定语从句；过去完成时 had described
译文：一次在澳大利亚两座偏远岛屿附近进行的考察，刚刚揭示了还有多少生命有待被发现——那是科学界此前从未描述过的 149 个物种。
Led by marine biologists from Australia's leading research institutions, the voyage targeted a chain of seamounts — ancient underwater volcanoes — rising from the floor of the Indian Ocean near Christmas Island and the Cocos (Keeling) Islands, a territory that lies far from any continent.
主干：the voyage targeted a chain of seamounts（主语 + targeted + 宾语）
过去分词作状语：Led by marine biologists from Australia's leading research institutions（"由……率领"）
同位语：ancient underwater volcanoes（破折号引出，解释 seamounts）
现在分词作定语：rising from the floor of the Indian Ocean near ...（修饰 seamounts）
同位语 + 定语从句：a territory that lies far from any continent（解释 Christmas Island and the Cocos Islands，that 引导定语从句）
考点：过去分词作状语；同位语；现在分词作后置定语；that 定语从句
译文：这次航行由澳大利亚顶尖研究机构的海洋生物学家率领，目标是一连串海底山——即古老的海底火山——它们从圣诞岛和科科斯（基林）群岛附近的印度洋洋底升起，而这片领地远离任何大陆。
What the team brought back astonished even seasoned researchers: among the 149 new species were a sea cucumber belonging to the genus Oneirophanta, a greeneye fish from the family Chlorophthalmus, and a parasitic creature that attaches itself to other deep-sea animals.
主语（名词性从句）：What the team brought back（what 引导的主语从句）
主干：What the team brought back astonished even seasoned researchers（主语 + astonished + 宾语）
倒装句：among the 149 new species were a sea cucumber ...（表语 among ... 提前的完全倒装，主语为并列的三个名词短语）
现在分词作定语：belonging to the genus Oneirophanta（修饰 sea cucumber）；that attaches itself to ...（定语从句修饰 creature）
考点：what 主语从句；完全倒装；现在分词作后置定语；that 定语从句
译文：考察队带回的东西令经验丰富的研究人员也惊叹不已：在这 149 个新物种中，有一种梦参属的海参、一种青眼鱼科的鱼，以及一种附着在其他深海动物身上的寄生生物。
To reach depths where sunlight never penetrates, the scientists relied on remotely operated vehicles and baited cameras, which descended thousands of metres to film and collect specimens from habitats that had never before been disturbed by human hands.
主干：the scientists relied on remotely operated vehicles and baited cameras（主语 + relied on + 宾语）
目的状语：To reach depths where sunlight never penetrates（不定式作目的状语，内含 where 引导的定语从句修饰 depths）
非限制性定语从句：which descended thousands of metres to film and collect specimens ...（which 修饰 vehicles and cameras）
定语从句：that had never before been disturbed by human hands（修饰 habitats，被动语态 + 过去完成时）
考点：不定式作目的状语；where / which / that 三类定语从句的区分；被动语态
译文：为了到达阳光永远无法穿透的深度，科学家依靠遥控潜水器和诱饵相机，它们下潜数千米，从从未被人类之手扰动过的栖息地拍摄并采集标本。
Deep-sea bottom trawling and seabed mining, both of which are expanding as resources on land grow scarce, could destroy these fragile ecosystems long before most of their inhabitants have even been named.
主干：Deep-sea bottom trawling and seabed mining ... could destroy these fragile ecosystems（主语 + could destroy + 宾语）
非限制性定语从句：both of which are expanding（which 指代前面的两个并列主语，both of which 表示"两者都"）
原因/时间状语从句：as resources on land grow scarce（as 引导，表"随着"）
时间状语从句：long before most of their inhabitants have even been named（long before 引导，表"远在……之前"）
考点：both of which 非限制性定语从句；as 引导状语从句；long before 时间状语从句；could 表推测
译文：随着陆地上的资源日益稀缺，深海底拖网捕捞和海底采矿都在不断扩张，而这两者可能在大多数栖息者尚未被命名之前，就摧毁这些脆弱的生态系统。

四、全文大意

海洋覆盖了地球超过三分之二的表面，但深海至今仍是人类探索最少的"最后疆域"。一支由澳大利亚顶尖研究机构海洋生物学家率领的考察队，把目标锁定在圣诞岛和科科斯（基林）群岛附近、远离任何大陆的一连串海底山——这些古老的海底火山从印度洋洋底升起，是散布在荒芜深海平原上的"生命孤岛"。科学家借助遥控潜水器和诱饵相机下潜数千米，从从未被人类扰动过的栖息地采集标本，一举带回 149 个科学界从未描述过的新物种，其中包括一种梦参属的海参、一种青眼鱼科的鱼，以及一种附着在其他深海动物身上的寄生生物。这些海底山在数百万年的隔绝中，让无数生物演化出地球上独一无二的特征，但正是这种隔绝让它们格外脆弱：随着陆地资源日益稀缺，底拖网捕捞和海底采矿不断扩张，可能在多数"居民"被正式命名之前就摧毁这些生态系统。为新物种正式命名往往需要数年细致研究，因此保护显得刻不容缓。这项发表于多篇科学论文的发现，是全球海洋生物编目行动的一部分——科学家估计海洋中可能有多达 200 万种生物，其中大多数仍不为人知。对普通人而言，这一发现既令人谦卑又充满希望：一次航行就能揭示如此之多的未知，说明海洋尽管承受着日益增长的压力，仍蕴藏着足以激励新一代探索者和自然保护者的奇迹。
