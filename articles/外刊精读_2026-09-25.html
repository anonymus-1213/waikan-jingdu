近视的"静默流行病"：屏幕时代的孩子们为何越来越看不清远方

A mounting body of evidence links the global rise in childhood myopia to too much near work and too little daylight — and suggests the cure may be as simple as going outside
来源 The Guardian / The Lancet / Brien Holden Vision Institute（综合）作者 外刊精读专栏日期 2026-09-25难度 难

中文导读

人们常把近视当作一副眼镜就能解决的小事，但一组权威的流行病学数据正把这件事推向公共健康的聚光灯下：到本世纪中叶，全球或将有近一半人口近视，而东亚城市的青少年近视率已高达八九成。本文梳理了关于"近视静默流行"的系统研究，指出真正的推手并非基因这一单一因素，而是现代童年里两股交织的习惯——无休止的近距离用眼，与户外日照的急剧萎缩。研究揭示了一条耐人寻味的保护机制：明亮的户外光促使视网膜释放多巴胺，从而抑制眼轴的过度拉长；而昏暗的室内与近距离屏幕，恰恰不给眼睛踩下这道"刹车"。更紧迫的是，高度近视会显著抬高视网膜脱离、青光眼与黄斑变性等致盲风险。好消息是，预防既便宜又有据可依——每天两小时户外、"20-20-20"用眼法、低剂量阿托品——只是这道"户外处方"对许多家庭而言，仍是一份奢侈。

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

【1】Far removed from any infectious outbreak, a quiet epidemic has been creeping through the world's classrooms, and it has nothing to do with viruses. myopia, the failure to bring distant objects into focus, already blurs the vision of one person in three — a share that may approach one in two by the middle of this century.
【2】The projections come from a landmark review by the Brien Holden Vision Institute, whose modelling, published in the journal Ophthalmology, drew on 145 studies spanning nearly 2.1 million participants. Its conclusion was blunt: within a generation, myopia could afflict some five billion people, a major public health concern.
【3】Nowhere is the shift more striking than in urban East Asia, where 80 to 90 percent of teenagers finishing secondary school are myopic. Researchers increasingly attribute the surge not to genes alone but to two interlocking habits of modern childhood — relentless near work and a sharp decline in time spent outdoors.
【4】The mechanism, though still debated, centres on light. Bright outdoor light, many orders of magnitude stronger than indoor lighting, is thought to prompt the retina to release dopamine, which in turn restrains the excessive elongation of the eyeball that defines myopia; dim rooms and close screens offer the eye no such brake.
【5】The COVID-19 lockdowns compounded the problem. With schools shut and lives grown sedentary, children's screen time soared while their daylight hours collapsed, and cohort studies recorded a sharp acceleration in the onset of myopia among the youngest.
【6】The costs extend far beyond thicker lenses. High myopia raises the lifetime risk of retinal detachment, glaucoma and macular degeneration, conditions that can lead to irreversible blindness, turning a correctable inconvenience into a lifelong burden on individuals and health systems alike.
【7】Prevention, the experts insist, is both cheap and well established. Roughly two hours of outdoor time each day, whatever the weather, can meaningfully curb the onset of myopia, while simple routines such as the "20-20-20" rule are now widely recommended; in much of the world, however, even a basic pair of spectacles remains beyond reach — let alone the atropine drops that wealthier families take for granted.
【8】Sceptics caution that most of the evidence is observational, so the direction of causality is not fully proven, and heredity remains a powerful determinant. Yet even the most guarded reviewers concede that the balance of evidence favours a protective role for daylight — a conclusion few parents would find costly to accept.
【9】The message, then, is a hopeful one: a largely outdoor remedy for a thoroughly indoor condition. That so simple a prescription — more daylight, less screen — could shield an entire generation of eyes suggests that the fight against the myopia epidemic may be won one recess at a time.

段落主旨

  1. 近视是一场与病毒无关、正悄然蔓延的"静默流行病"，约三分之一人口已受影响。
  2. 权威综述基于 145 项研究、210 万参与者建模，预测一代人内近视将波及 50 亿人。
  3. 东亚城市青少年近视率高达八九成，主因是近距离用眼与户外时间骤减。
  4. 机理在"光"：强光促使视网膜释放多巴胺，抑制眼轴过度拉长。
  5. 疫情封控加剧问题：屏幕时间飙升、日照锐减，儿童近视加速发生。
  6. 高度近视推高视网膜脱离、青光眼与黄斑变性风险，可能致盲。
  7. 每天两小时户外与"20-20-20"规则等预防手段廉价有效，但部分地区连眼镜都难获得。
  8. 证据多为观察性、因果未完全证实，但综合证据仍支持"日光保护"。
  9. 启示：一副"户外处方"或能以每次课间一点一滴赢得这场战役。

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

Nowhere is the shift more striking than in urban East Asia, where 80 to 90 percent of teenagers finishing secondary school are myopic.
主干：Nowhere is the shift more striking（倒装句：否定副词 nowhere 置于句首，部分倒装 is + 主语）
比较结构：more striking than in urban East Asia（"比东亚城市地区更显著"）
非限制性定语从句：where 80 to 90 percent of teenagers … are myopic（where 指代 in urban East Asia）
后置定语（现在分词短语）：finishing secondary school（修饰 teenagers）
考点：否定词置句首的部分倒装（语法填空常考）；现在分词作后置定语；where 引导非限制性定语从句
译文：这一转变在东亚城市地区最为显著，那里 80% 到 90% 的、即将完成中学学业的青少年都是近视眼。
The projections come from a landmark review by the Brien Holden Vision Institute, whose modelling, published in the journal Ophthalmology, drew on 145 studies spanning nearly 2.1 million participants.
主干：The projections come from a landmark review（主语 + come from + 宾语）
定语从句（whose）：whose modelling … drew on 145 studies（whose 指代 the Brien Holden Vision Institute）
过去分词作定语：published in the journal Ophthalmology（修饰 modelling，插入在从句主语与谓语之间）
现在分词作定语：spanning nearly 2.1 million participants（修饰 145 studies）
考点：whose 引导定语从句；过去分词 / 现在分词作定语（doing/done 的选择）；draw on 搭配
译文：这些预测来自布莱恩·霍尔登视觉研究所的一项里程碑式综述，该研究所发表《眼科学》杂志上的建模利用了涵盖近 210 万名参与者的 145 项研究。
Bright outdoor light, many orders of magnitude stronger than indoor lighting, is thought to prompt the retina to release dopamine, which in turn restrains the excessive elongation of the eyeball that defines myopia.
主干：Bright outdoor light … is thought to prompt the retina to release dopamine（主语 + 被动 is thought + 不定式）
插入语（比较短语）：many orders of magnitude stronger than indoor lighting（补充说明 outdoor light 的强度）
非限制性定语从句：which in turn restrains the excessive elongation …（which 指代 dopamine 释放这一行为）
限制性定语从句：that defines myopia（修饰 the excessive elongation of the eyeball）
考点：be thought to do 被动结构；which 非限制性 / that 限制性定语从句的区别；名词化 the elongation of …
译文：比室内照明强出许多个数量级的明亮户外光，被认为能促使视网膜释放多巴胺，而多巴胺反过来又抑制了导致近视的眼球过度拉长。
High myopia raises the lifetime risk of retinal detachment, glaucoma and macular degeneration, conditions that can lead to irreversible blindness, turning a correctable inconvenience into a lifelong burden on individuals and health systems alike.
主干：High myopia raises the lifetime risk（主语 + raises + 宾语）
介词短语作定语：of retinal detachment, glaucoma and macular degeneration（修饰 risk）
同位语 + 定语从句：conditions that can lead to irreversible blindness（conditions 概括前面三种眼病，that 引导定语从句）
现在分词作结果状语：turning a correctable inconvenience into a lifelong burden（"从而把……变成……"）
考点：同位语；that 定语从句；现在分词短语作结果状语；turn A into B 搭配
译文：高度近视会提高视网膜脱离、青光眼和黄斑变性这几种疾病的终生患病风险，这些疾病可能导致不可逆的失明，从而把一种可矫正的小麻烦变成压在个人与整个卫生系统身上的终身重担。
That so simple a prescription — more daylight, less screen — could shield an entire generation of eyes suggests that the fight against the myopia epidemic may be won one recess at a time.
主干：That … could shield an entire generation of eyes suggests that …（That 引导主语从句 + suggests + that 宾语从句）
主语从句：That so simple a prescription … could shield an entire generation of eyes（that 引导，作 suggests 的主语）
插入语（同位语）：more daylight, less screen（破折号引出，解释 prescription）
宾语从句：that the fight against the myopia epidemic may be won one recess at a time（that 引导，作 suggests 的宾语）
考点：that 引导主语从句；that 引导宾语从句；插入语；one … at a time 结构
译文：如此简单的一纸"处方"——多一点日光、少一点屏幕——竟能庇护整整一代人的眼睛，这表明对抗近视流行病的战斗，或许可以靠每一次课间休息一点点地打赢。

四、全文大意

近视常被视作一副眼镜就能解决的小问题，但一组权威流行病学数据正在把它推向公共健康舞台的中央。文章开篇指出，一场与病毒无关、正悄然蔓延的"静默流行病"已让约三分之一人口视力模糊，而这一比例到本世纪中叶或逼近二分之一。这一预测来自布莱恩·霍尔登视觉研究所发表于《眼科学》杂志的里程碑式综述——它综合了 145 项研究、近 210 万名参与者的数据，结论直白：一代人之内近视可能波及约 50 亿人。转变最显著的地方是东亚城市，八九成青少年在完成中学学业时已是近视眼；研究者越来越倾向于把这一激增归因于现代童年的两股交织习惯——无休止的近距离用眼，以及户外日照的急剧萎缩。其机理虽仍在争论，但核心在"光"：比室内强许多个数量级的户外光促使视网膜释放多巴胺，从而抑制导致近视的眼球过度拉长，而昏暗房间与近距离屏幕恰恰不给眼睛踩下这道刹车。疫情封控使问题雪上加霜——学校关闭、生活久坐，屏幕时间飙升、日照锐减，多项队列研究记录了低龄儿童近视发生与进展的急剧加速。代价远不止更厚的镜片：高度近视抬高视网膜脱离、青光眼与黄斑变性的终生风险，这些疾病可能致盲，把可矫正的小麻烦变成个人与卫生系统的终身负担。所幸预防既便宜又有据可依——每天两小时户外、"20-20-20"用眼法、低剂量阿托品均被证实有效；然而在世界许多地方，一副普通眼镜尚且难求，更不用说富裕家庭习以为常的阿托品滴剂。批评者提醒，多数证据是观察性的，因果方向尚未完全证实，遗传仍是强大决定因素；但即便是最谨慎的评论者也承认，证据的天平倾向"日光具有保护作用"。文章的结语充满希望：这是一副"以户外治室内"的处方，而它或许能以每一次课间、每一点日光的累积，一点点赢得这场战役。
