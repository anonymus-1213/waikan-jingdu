AI 热度降温，学生仍想要更聪明的学习工具

AI hype fades, but students still want smarter study tools
来源 Stacker（源自 Quizlet《How America Learns Report 2026》）作者 Kurt Beilder日期 2026-09-05难度 中等

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

1The early enthusiasm around artificial intelligence in education is cooling. According to Quizlet's second annual "How America Learns" report, only 30 percent of students and teachers now say they feel positive — excited or optimistic — about AI in the classroom, down from 43 percent a year earlier.
2Yet the same survey reveals a striking contradiction: even as enthusiasm fades, students are not turning away from AI. They are simply demanding better tools. Many students who use AI report real benefits, including saving time and getting faster explanations.
3"Students are no longer impressed by AI just because it is AI," researchers note. What they want are study tools that adapt to how they actually learn — tools that can explain, quiz, and coach them in a personalized way.
4Skepticism is growing among teachers and parents too. Many worry that students may rely on AI to do their work for them rather than to learn, and they want clearer guidance on how to use AI responsibly.
5The report found that students who use AI regularly tend to study more, not less. More than half of K-12 students say they use AI for homework help, and a similar share of college students use it to save time.
6Experts say the message is clear: the "wow" factor has worn off, but the need for smart, adaptive learning tools is stronger than ever. Companies are responding by building features that focus on practice, feedback, and understanding rather than quick answers.
7The challenge now is to turn a tool of convenience into a tool of genuine learning — and to make sure that as AI becomes a normal part of studying, it strengthens students' skills instead of replacing them.

原文出处：Stacker 商业报道（作者 Kurt Beilder，2026-09）。素材源自 Quizlet 第二份年度报告《How America Learns Report 2026》，本文为根据公开转载内容整理的精读版。

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

Yet the same survey reveals a striking contradiction: even as enthusiasm fades, students are not turning away from AI.
主干：the same survey reveals a striking contradiction
说明成分：a striking contradiction: … —— 冒号后对"矛盾"作具体说明
让步状语从句：even as enthusiasm fades（as 引导时间/让步，"尽管热情消退"）
并列主句：students are not turning away from AI（turn away from 意为"离开、背弃"）
译文：然而同一项调查揭示了一个鲜明的矛盾：尽管热情在消退，学生们并没有离开 AI。
What they want are study tools that adapt to how they actually learn — tools that can explain, quiz, and coach them in a personalized way.
主语从句：What they want（"他们想要的"，作整个句子的主语）
主干：(What they want) are study tools
定语从句①：that adapt to how they actually learn —— 修饰 tools，内含宾语从句 how they actually learn
破折号补充：tools that can explain, quiz, and coach them … —— 对 study tools 进一步说明，内含三个并列动词
译文：他们想要的是能够适应自己实际学习方式的学习工具——能够以个性化的方式讲解、测验并辅导他们的工具。
Many worry that students may rely on AI to do their work for them rather than to learn.
主干：Many (people) worry
宾语从句：that students may rely on AI（worry 的宾语，说明担忧的内容）
目的状语：to do their work for them —— 不定式表目的
对比结构：rather than to learn —— rather than 连接两个不定式，表"而不是"
译文：许多人担心，学生可能会依赖 AI 替自己完成作业，而不是借助它来学习。
The report found that students who use AI regularly tend to study more, not less.
主干：The report found
宾语从句：that students … tend to study more（found 的宾语）
定语从句：who use AI regularly —— 修饰 students
对比结构：more, not less —— 省略式对比，加强语气
译文：报告发现，经常使用 AI 的学生往往会学得更多，而非更少。
The challenge now is to turn a tool of convenience into a tool of genuine learning — and to make sure that as AI becomes a normal part of studying, it strengthens students' skills instead of replacing them.
主干：The challenge now is to turn a tool of convenience into a tool of genuine learning
并列不定式：and to make sure that …（与 is to turn … 并列）
宾语从句：that … it strengthens students' skills instead of replacing them
时间状语从句：as AI becomes a normal part of studying（as 引导，"随着…"）
译文：现在的挑战，是把一件便捷工具变成真正的学习工具——并确保随着 AI 成为学习中的常态，它能增强而非取代学生的能力。

四、全文大意

教育领域对人工智能的早期狂热正在降温：Quizlet 第二份年度报告《How America Learns》显示，对 AI 持正面态度的师生比例已从去年的 43% 降到 30%。但调查同时揭示了一个鲜明矛盾——尽管热情消退，学生并未离开 AI，而是提出更务实的要求：他们想要能真正适应自己学习方式的智能工具，能在讲解、测验、辅导上做到个性化。教师与家长的疑虑也在上升，担心学生借 AI 代劳而非学习，因而呼吁更清晰的使用规范。报告指出，经常使用 AI 的学生往往学得更多而非更少，超过半数中小学生用 AI 求助作业，过半大学生用它节省时间。专家认为，"新鲜感"已经褪去，但对聪明、自适应工具的需求比以往更强烈，真正的挑战在于让 AI 增强而非取代学生的能力。

五、思考与仿写

思考题：报道指出学生"不再因为 AI 是 AI 而被打动"，却仍想要更聪明的学习工具。请结合第 3、5 段，谈谈当新鲜感降温后，学生对 AI 工具的要求反而更务实了，这对"技术与学习的关系"意味着什么？
仿写题：仿照第 2 段的让步句式 "even as … fades, … are not turning away from …"，围绕「纸质书」或「传统课堂」写一个句子；再用第 3 段的 "What they want are … that …" 主语从句句式，写一句关于「理想中的学习方式」的话。
