减肥药正在拉开新的健康差距

Only high earners make overall savings on food bills from weight-loss drugs
来源 Financial Times（金融时报）作者 外刊精读专栏日期 2026-09-09难度 较高

中文导读

风靡全球的 GLP-1 减肥药，正悄悄成为一道新的"健康分水岭"。《金融时报》援引咨询公司 Baringa 的分析指出：这类药物的高昂费用，实际上是对"变瘦"征收的一种累退税——除最高收入人群外，买药的开销远超省下的伙食费。一名使用者需年均可支配收入接近 10 万英镑，才能勉强在扣除每年 1200 英镑药费后真正"省钱"。更令人担忧的是，高昂药价已让部分人中途停药、体重反弹，甚至可能为维持用药而负债。本文梳理了这组数据背后的公平隐忧，并追问：当健康被标价，谁被排除在外？

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

【1】The high cost of weight-loss medication is a "regressive tax on being thin" because the outlay outweighs any saving on food costs for all but the highest earners.
【2】Someone needs to have discretionary income of nearly £100,000 a year to actually save money after covering the £1,200 annual cost of GLP-1 medication, according to an analysis by the consultancy Baringa.
【3】"Our analysis shows that GLP-1s risk becoming a driver of inequality," said Paddy Winters, a partner at Baringa. "Only very high earners will benefit from savings to their groceries whilst taking the GLP-1 pill."
【4】A regressive tax is one that takes a larger percentage from lower-wage earners' income compared with those who are more wealthy. Most users buy the drugs with a private prescription.
【5】A GLP-1 user with £39,000 left over after tax and essentials will spend £481 less a year on food. But it is not until someone has £97,500 left to spend after tax and other outgoings that their grocery spend is reduced by £1,200, according to Baringa. The cost is an average for the pill form of the medication.
【6】For "everyone else" the annual cost of the drug outweighs any reduction in spending on groceries, Winters said. "On top of that, users are spending more on vitamin supplements and personal care products like hair care, to manage side effects. In effect, GLP-1s become a regressive tax on being thin."
【7】"We're already seeing early signs that some people cancel their prescription after a few months because the cost adds up — but the weight comes back once you stop," he added. "So the pattern is likely to be start, stop, regain and return, and to fund that cycle, we may well see people turn to debt."
【8】There are 14.3 million adults living with obesity in England, according to the King's Fund. In a recent report, the charity said the government "needs to ensure that the people who benefit from [weight-loss medication] most are not priced out of accessing them, and that we don't end up with wider health inequalities caused by obesity due to affordability and access".

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

The high cost of weight-loss medication is a "regressive tax on being thin" because the outlay outweighs any saving on food costs for all but the highest earners.
主干：The high cost … is a "regressive tax on being thin"（主语 + 系表结构）
原因状语从句：because the outlay outweighs any saving on food costs for all but the highest earners
介词短语：for all but the highest earners（all but = "除……外几乎全部"，此处指"几乎全部，但最高收入者除外"）
考点：because 引导原因状语从句；all but 固定短语表"几乎全部"
译文：减肥药的高昂费用，是对"变瘦"征收的一种累退税，因为除了最高收入人群外，买药的支出都超过了省下的伙食费。
Someone needs to have discretionary income of nearly £100,000 a year to actually save money after covering the £1,200 annual cost of GLP-1 medication, according to an analysis by the consultancy Baringa.
主干：Someone needs to have discretionary income of nearly £100,000 a year（主语 + need to do）
目的状语（不定式）：to actually save money after covering the £1,200 annual cost（after + 动名词 covering）
来源状语：according to an analysis by the consultancy Baringa（according to 介词短语）
考点：不定式作目的状语；介词 after + 动名词；according to 引出来源
译文：根据咨询公司 Baringa 的分析，一个人需要年均可自由支配收入接近 10 万英镑，才能在支付 GLP-1 药物每年 1200 英镑的费用后真正省钱。
A regressive tax is one that takes a larger percentage from lower-wage earners' income compared with those who are more wealthy.
主干：A regressive tax is one（主语 + 系表结构，one 指代 a tax）
定语从句 1：that takes a larger percentage from lower-wage earners' income（修饰 one）
过去分词短语：compared with those who are more wealthy（compared with 表被动/比较）
定语从句 2：who are more wealthy（修饰 those）
考点：that / who 引导定语从句；compared with 过去分词短语作状语
译文：累退税是一种从低收入者收入中抽取更大比例的税，与更富裕人群相比形成反差。
But it is not until someone has £97,500 left to spend after tax and other outgoings that their grocery spend is reduced by £1,200, according to Baringa.
强调句型：it is not until … that …（not until 的强调句式，"直到……才……"）
被强调的时间状语：until someone has £97,500 left to spend after tax and other outgoings（内含 have sth left 结构）
主句：their grocery spend is reduced by £1,200（被动语态 is reduced）
考点：not until 的强调句型；have sth left；被动语态
译文：但根据 Baringa 的数据，要直到一个人在缴税和其他开支后还剩下 9.75 万英镑可花时，其伙食开支才会减少 1200 英镑。
In a recent report, the charity said the government "needs to ensure that the people who benefit from [weight-loss medication] most are not priced out of accessing them, and that we don't end up with wider health inequalities caused by obesity due to affordability and access".
主干：the charity said the government "needs to ensure that … and that …"（said 后接省略 that 的宾语从句）
宾语从句 1：the people who benefit from [weight-loss medication] most are not priced out of accessing them（who 引导定语从句修饰 people）
宾语从句 2：we don't end up with wider health inequalities caused by obesity（caused by 过去分词短语作后置定语）
介词短语：due to affordability and access（表原因）
考点：宾语从句（ensure that … and that …）；who 引导定语从句；过去分词 caused by 作后置定语；due to 表原因
译文：在最近的一份报告中，该慈善机构表示，政府"需要确保那些最能从减肥药中受益的人不会因价格过高而无法获得治疗，并确保我们不会因负担能力与可及性问题，最终出现由肥胖引起的更广泛的健康不平等"。

四、全文大意

《金融时报》援引咨询公司 Baringa 的分析指出，GLP-1 减肥药的高昂费用，实际上构成了一种面向"变瘦"的累退税——除最高收入人群外，买药的支出都超过了省下的伙食费。数据显示，一个人需要年均可自由支配收入接近 10 万英镑，才能在支付每年 1200 英镑药费后真正"省钱"；收入越低的用药者，省下的伙食费越少，甚至入不敷出。Baringa 合伙人 Paddy Winters 直言，GLP-1 正有成为"不平等推手"的风险。除了药价，使用者还要额外购买维生素补充剂和护发等个人护理产品来应对副作用，进一步加重负担。更严峻的是，已有人因费用累积而在数月后停药，体重随之反弹，形成"开始—停药—反弹—复购"的循环，甚至可能为维持用药而转向负债。英国有 1430 万成年人患有肥胖症，慈善机构国王基金会呼吁政府确保最需要的人不被价格排斥，避免因负担能力与可及性差异而加剧健康不平等。文章提醒我们：当健康被标上价格，公平这道门槛正在悄悄抬高。

五、思考与仿写
