珊瑚礁的"避风港"：科学家找到能在变暖中活下来的礁石

A new study maps 166,000 square kilometres of coral reef that could survive — and even recover from — climate change
来源 Reuters / The Independent（综合）作者 外刊精读专栏日期 2026-09-09难度 中等

中文导读

珊瑚礁被称为"海洋中的热带雨林"——它们只覆盖了不到 1% 的海底，却养育着约四分之一的海洋物种。随着海水变暖，大片珊瑚正在白化、死亡。但一项新研究带来了难得的好消息：科学家利用卫星数据与计算机模型，在全球找出约 16.6 万平方公里的珊瑚礁，它们因位于罕见的"凉爽地带"或进化出耐热能力，有望在气候变化中存活甚至恢复。这份"幸存地图"将帮助各国锁定保护重点，也提醒我们：珊瑚礁的未来，并非一片黑暗。

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

【1】Coral reefs are often called the "rainforests of the sea". They cover less than one percent of the ocean floor, yet they are home to about a quarter of all marine species.
【2】But these colourful underwater cities are in trouble. As the ocean grows warmer, many corals turn white and die in a process called bleaching. Scientists have long feared that most reefs might disappear within a few decades.
【3】Now, a new study offers a rare piece of good news. An international team of researchers has identified about 166,000 square kilometres of coral reef — an area larger than many countries — that could survive and even recover from climate change.
【4】The team used satellite data and computer models to find reefs that are likely to withstand rising temperatures. Some of these "bright spots" sit in rare cool places, where deep, cool water rises to protect the corals from heat.
【5】Others have survived because their corals have evolved to tolerate warm water. "These reefs are not doomed," said the lead researcher. "Many of them can bounce back if we give them a chance."
【6】The findings matter greatly, because coral reefs are not just beautiful. They protect coastlines from storms and provide food and jobs for hundreds of millions of people around the world.
【7】Researchers say the new map will help governments and conservation groups decide where to focus their efforts. Protecting these resilient reefs should now be a top priority.
【8】The study also carries a clear message: the future of coral reefs is not all dark. With careful protection, at least some of these underwater treasures can be saved for future generations.

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

An international team of researchers has identified about 166,000 square kilometres of coral reef — an area larger than many countries — that could survive and even recover from climate change.
主干：An international team of researchers has identified about 166,000 square kilometres of coral reef（主语 + has identified + 宾语）
插入语（同位语）：an area larger than many countries（对前面面积的补充说明）
定语从句：that could survive and even recover from climate change（修饰 coral reef）
考点：现在完成时 has identified；that 引导限制性定语从句；同位语作插入补充
译文：一个国际研究团队识别出约 16.6 万平方公里的珊瑚礁——这个面积比许多国家还要大——它们能够在气候变化中存活甚至恢复。
Some of these "bright spots" sit in rare cool places, where deep, cool water rises to protect the corals from heat.
主干：Some of these "bright spots" sit in rare cool places（主语 + sit + 地点状语）
非限制性定语从句：where deep, cool water rises to protect the corals from heat（where 引导，修饰 places）
不定式短语：to protect the corals from heat（作目的状语）
考点：where 引导非限制性定语从句；protect … from … 固定搭配；不定式作目的状语
译文：这些"亮点"中，有一部分位于罕见的凉爽地带，那里深层凉爽的海水上升，使珊瑚免受高温侵害。
Others have survived because their corals have evolved to tolerate warm water.
主干：Others have survived（主语 + 现在完成时谓语）
原因状语从句：because their corals have evolved to tolerate warm water
不定式短语：to tolerate warm water（作 evolved 的目的/结果状语）
考点：because 引导原因状语从句；现在完成时 have evolved；不定式 to tolerate
译文：另一些珊瑚礁之所以存活下来，是因为其中的珊瑚已经进化出了耐受温暖海水的能力。
They protect coastlines from storms and provide food and jobs for hundreds of millions of people around the world.
主干：They protect coastlines from storms and provide food and jobs（并列谓语 protect … and provide …）
谓语 1：protect coastlines from storms（protect A from B）
谓语 2：provide food and jobs for hundreds of millions of people（provide sth for sb）
考点：and 连接并列谓语；protect … from …；provide sth for sb
译文：它们保护海岸线免受风暴侵袭，并为全世界数以亿计的人们提供食物和就业机会。
With careful protection, at least some of these underwater treasures can be saved for future generations.
主干：at least some of these underwater treasures can be saved（主语 + 情态动词被动语态）
方式状语：With careful protection（with + 名词，作伴随/方式状语）
时间/对象状语：for future generations（为了后代）
考点：情态动词 can 的被动语态 can be saved；with 引导方式状语
译文：在精心的保护之下，这些海底宝藏中至少有一部分能够为子孙后代留存下来。

四、全文大意

珊瑚礁被誉为"海洋中的热带雨林"，虽然只覆盖不到 1% 的海底，却养育着约四分之一的海洋物种。然而，随着海水变暖，珊瑚白化现象加剧，科学家一度担心多数礁石会在几十年内消失。一项新研究带来了转机：一个国际团队利用卫星数据和计算机模型，在全球识别出约 16.6 万平方公里的珊瑚礁，它们有望在气候变化中存活甚至恢复——有的位于罕见的"凉爽地带"，深层凉水上升保护珊瑚免受高温；有的则因珊瑚已进化出耐热能力而幸存。研究意义重大：珊瑚礁不仅美观，还能护岸防浪、为全球数亿人提供食物与生计。这份"幸存地图"将帮助各国政府和环保组织锁定保护重点，把保护这些"有复原力"的礁石列为优先事项。研究也传递出一个清晰信号：珊瑚礁的未来并非一片黑暗，只要精心保护，这些海底宝藏至少有一部分能留给后代。
