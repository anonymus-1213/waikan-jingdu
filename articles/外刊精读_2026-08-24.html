OpenAI 因 SpaceX 收购终止向 Cursor 供应模型

OpenAI to stop supplying models to Cursor after SpaceX acquisition
来源 The Next Web (TNW)作者 未署名日期 2026-08-24难度 中等

中文导读

OpenAI 宣布，因 SpaceX 以 600 亿美元全股票收购 AI 编程助手 Cursor，将于 11 月 12 日终止向 Cursor 供应模型，也不再提供下一代 Astra 模型。给出的理由直指信任：基于"与马斯克旗下公司违反合同的过往经历"，OpenAI 无法确信 SpaceX 会在服务条款范围内使用其技术。本文梳理了断供的理由、收购背景与最长通知期，并指出真正的代价落在依赖平台的开发者身上——而欧洲现行的云切换规则，也未必能覆盖"供应商主动退出"的情形。

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

【1】OpenAI is ending its contract to supply models to Cursor. The shutoff date is 12 November, the company said on Saturday.
【2】The reason given is unusually direct. OpenAI said it cannot be confident SpaceX will use its technology within its terms of service, "based on our experience with Elon Musk's companies violating contracts".
【3】Bloomberg reported it first. OpenAI published the same notice on X and on its own website.
【4】Two grounds are named, both about earlier conduct. SpaceX's acquisition of Twitter breached OpenAI's terms, the company said, and xAI violated them in a way Musk admitted under oath this year.
【5】The buyer completed its purchase this month. SpaceX closed the $60B all-stock acquisition of Cursor after agreeing it in June.
【6】OpenAI says it gave the maximum notice its contract allows, about two and a half months. Astra, its next model, will not be supplied to Cursor at all. It added that it is ready to go above and beyond to support developers moving off.
【7】The leverage in that is thinner than it looks. Cursor and SpaceXAI shipped Grok 4.5 together in July, a model Musk called Opus-class, available in Cursor on every plan.
【8】What it costs developers is real. Europe wrote rules for exactly this dependency, and since September 2025 cloud customers have been able to switch on two months' notice and port their assets within 30 days. From January 2027 the provider cannot charge for any of it.
【9】There is a further gap in them. The switching rules protect a customer who wants to leave a provider, not a customer whose provider decides to leave. Nobody drafted for a supplier walking out.

原文出处：The Next Web（TNW），2026-08-29。Bloomberg 率先报道，OpenAI 同日于 X 及官网发布相同公告。

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

OpenAI said it cannot be confident SpaceX will use its technology within its terms of service, "based on our experience with Elon Musk's companies violating contracts".
主干：OpenAI said …
宾语从句①：it cannot be confident …（said 的宾语，省略 that）
宾语从句②：SpaceX will use its technology within its terms of service —— 作 confident 的补充说明（形容词后的从句）
原因状语：based on our experience … —— 过去分词短语；with Elon Musk's companies violating contracts 为"with + 名词 + 现在分词"复合结构
译文：OpenAI 表示，鉴于"与马斯克旗下公司违反合同的过往经历"，它无法确信 SpaceX 会在其服务条款范围内使用这项技术。
Two grounds are named, both about earlier conduct. SpaceX's acquisition of Twitter breached OpenAI's terms, the company said, and xAI violated them in a way Musk admitted under oath this year.
主干①：Two grounds are named（两个理由被列出）
同位语：both about earlier conduct —— 补充说明"两项理由均涉及此前的行为"
主干②：SpaceX's acquisition of Twitter breached OpenAI's terms, and xAI violated them —— 并列句
插入语：the company said —— 报道句式
定语从句：in a way (that) Musk admitted under oath this year —— 修饰 way，省略关系词 that
译文：公告列出了两条理由，均与此前的行为有关：SpaceX 收购 Twitter 违反了 OpenAI 的条款；而 xAI 的违规方式，马斯克今年已在宣誓作证时承认。
OpenAI says it gave the maximum notice its contract allows, about two and a half months.
主干：OpenAI says …
宾语从句：it gave the maximum notice（省略 that）
定语从句：(that) its contract allows —— 修饰 the maximum notice，省略关系词 that
同位语：about two and a half months —— 对"最长通知期"作进一步说明
译文：OpenAI 表示，它已给出合同所允许的最长通知期——约两个半月。
Europe wrote rules for exactly this dependency, and since September 2025 cloud customers have been able to switch on two months' notice and port their assets within 30 days.
并列句：Europe wrote rules …, and … cloud customers have been able to switch … and port …
主干②谓语：have been able to switch … and (to) port their assets —— 并列的不定式
时间状语：since September 2025 —— 与完成时搭配
方式状语：on two months' notice（提前两个月通知）/ within 30 days（30 天内）
译文：欧洲正是针对这种依赖关系制定了规则：自 2025 年 9 月起，云客户可在提前两个月通知的情况下切换供应商，并在 30 天内迁移其资产。
The switching rules protect a customer who wants to leave a provider, not a customer whose provider decides to leave.
主干：The switching rules protect a customer …, not a customer …
对比结构：protect A, not B —— 强调规则保护的是前者而非后者
定语从句①：who wants to leave a provider —— 修饰第一个 customer（主动离开的客户）
定语从句②：whose provider decides to leave —— 修饰第二个 customer（供应商决定离开的客户）
译文：这些切换规则保护的是"想离开供应商"的客户，而不是"供应商决定离开"的客户。

四、全文大意

OpenAI 因 SpaceX 以 600 亿美元全股票收购 AI 编程助手 Cursor，决定终止向 Cursor 供应其模型，定于 11 月 12 日关停，并拒绝提供下一代 Astra 模型。OpenAI 给出的理由异常直接：基于"与马斯克旗下公司违反合同的过往经历"，无法确信 SpaceX 会在服务条款范围内使用其技术。公告列出两条先例——SpaceX 收购 Twitter 曾违反 OpenAI 条款，xAI 的违规行为马斯克今年已宣誓承认。OpenAI 称已按合同给予最长约两个半月的通知。不过报道指出，Cursor 已于 7 月与 SpaceXAI 合作推出 Grok 4.5，削弱了 OpenAI 此举的筹码；真正的代价落在开发者身上，而欧盟现有的云切换规则也未必能覆盖"供应商主动退出"的情形。

五、思考与仿写

思考题：报道称 OpenAI 终止合作的理由是"无法确信 SpaceX 会在条款范围内使用技术"。请结合第 2、4 段，谈谈"信任"在科技商业合作中的角色，以及平台依赖（dependency）给开发者带来的风险。
仿写题：仿照第 9 段的对比结构 "… protect a customer who …, not a customer whose …"，用 who / whose 两个定语从句，围绕「规则覆盖范围」或「选择权」写一个句子。
