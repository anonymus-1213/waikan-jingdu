座头鲸的歌声，原来和潜水"同步"：在不同深度唱不同的歌

Using suction-cup tags on 16 whales off Maui, researchers find that specific song themes match distinct phases of a whale's dive cycle
来源 Current Biology / Syracuse University / Earth.com（综合）作者 外刊精读专栏日期 2026-09-19难度 难

中文导读

座头鲸的歌声是动物界最复杂的声音之一——雄性会在繁殖季反复吟唱由呻吟、鸣叫与低吟构成的旋律，动辄数小时。但长久以来，海洋生物学家始终困惑：这头唱歌的鲸，在水下到底在做什么？雪城大学的一支团队在毛伊岛附近给 16 头鲸装上能同时记录声音与运动的吸盘式标签，抓取了数千次潜水及其伴随的歌声。结果发表在《当代生物学》上：鲸歌的"主题"与其潜水循环的阶段惊人地一一对应——下潜时唱一套主题，转身上升时唱另一套，最深处的潜水恰与最繁复的乐段相伴。这意味着鲸并非随机歌唱，而是围绕自身呼吸与运动的节奏编排曲目。这一发现为研究者打开了一扇"声学窗口"：只靠"听"，或许就能推断一头鲸在潜水中的位置与姿态。本文拆解这项研究的工具、发现、机理、局限与保护启示。

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

【1】The song of the humpback whale is among the most complex sounds in the animal kingdom, a series of moans, cries and rumbles that a singing male may repeat for hours on end. Yet for all its beauty, one question has long puzzled marine biologists: what, exactly, is a singing whale doing beneath the waves while it performs?
【2】A team of biologists at Syracuse University set out to answer that question with a tool that had never been applied in quite this way. Working off the island of Maui, they attached suction-cup tags, which record both sound and movement, to sixteen adult whales, capturing thousands of dives and the songs that accompanied them.
【3】The results, published in the journal Current Biology, revealed a remarkably tight correspondence between the structure of a whale's song and the stages of its dive. Specific song themes, the researchers found, are matched to specific phases of the dive cycle, as if each movement of the body were choreographed to a particular musical passage.
【4】As a whale descended, it sang one set of themes; as it turned and ascended toward the surface, it sang another. The deepest portions of a dive, in particular, were associated with the most elaborate segments of the song, suggesting that the animals were not merely singing at random but organising their repertoire around the rhythm of their own breath and motion.
【5】This link between sound and behaviour is more than a curiosity. Because singing whales are notoriously difficult to observe underwater, the discovery offers researchers a kind of acoustic window: by listening to which theme a whale is singing, they may be able to monitor where it is in a dive and how its body is moving, without ever seeing it.
【6】The finding also deepens our understanding of why whales sing at all. Humpback song is culturally transmitted — learned, modified and shared within populations — and its intricate structure is now shown to be inseparable from the physical act of diving, a feat that demands careful management of air and energy under crushing pressure.
【7】"The song is not just floating above the behaviour; it is woven into it," one of the researchers noted, adding that the correspondence could help scientists convey and track the physiological state of whales in places and at depths where visual tracking is impossible.
【8】The authors are careful to note the study's limits. The sample of sixteen whales came from a single breeding ground, and whether the same song-to-dive mapping holds for other populations, or for females and calves, remains an open question that will require far more listening.
【9】For those who study and love the ocean, the work carries a profound implication. It suggests that even the most familiar sounds of the sea still hold secrets, and that understanding them may be vital to protecting the elusive creatures that produce them — whales whose hidden lives we are only beginning to hear.

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

The song of the humpback whale is among the most complex sounds in the animal kingdom, a series of moans, cries and rumbles that a singing male may repeat for hours on end.
主干：The song ... is among the most complex sounds（主语 + is + 表语）
后置定语：of the humpback whale 修饰 song；in the animal kingdom 修饰 sounds
同位语 + 定语从句：a series of moans, cries and rumbles that a singing male may repeat for hours on end（解释 the song；that 引导定语从句）
考点：among the + 最高级；同位语；that 定语从句；for hours on end 短语
译文：座头鲸的歌声是动物界最复杂的声音之一——那是一连串由呻吟、鸣叫与低吟构成的旋律，一头歌唱的雄鲸可以连续重复数小时。
Working off the island of Maui, they attached suction-cup tags, which record both sound and movement, to sixteen adult whales, capturing thousands of dives and the songs that accompanied them.
主干：they attached suction-cup tags ... to sixteen adult whales（主语 + attached A to B）
现在分词作状语：Working off the island of Maui（"在毛伊岛附近工作"）
非限制性定语从句：which record both sound and movement（which 指代 tags）
现在分词作结果状语：capturing thousands of dives and the songs that accompanied them（内含 that 定语从句修饰 songs）
考点：现在分词作状语；attach A to B；非限制性定语从句；现在分词作结果状语
译文：在毛伊岛附近开展工作期间，他们把能同时记录声音与运动的吸盘式标签装到 16 头成年鲸身上，捕捉到了数千次潜水及其伴随的歌声。
Specific song themes, the researchers found, are matched to specific phases of the dive cycle, as if each movement of the body were choreographed to a particular musical passage.
主干：Specific song themes ... are matched to specific phases of the dive cycle（主语 + are matched to + 宾语）
插入语：the researchers found（"研究者发现"）
方式状语从句（虚拟语气）：as if each movement of the body were choreographed to a particular musical passage（as if 后用 were，表"仿佛"）
考点：插入语；被动语态 be matched to；as if 虚拟语气（were 表非真实）
译文：研究者发现，特定的乐句主题与潜水循环的特定阶段相匹配，仿佛身体的每一个动作都对应着某一段特定的旋律。
Because singing whales are notoriously difficult to observe underwater, the discovery offers researchers a kind of acoustic window: by listening to which theme a whale is singing, they may be able to monitor where it is in a dive and how its body is moving, without ever seeing it.
主干：the discovery offers researchers a kind of acoustic window（主语 + offers + 间接宾语 + 直接宾语）
原因状语从句：Because singing whales are notoriously difficult to observe underwater
同位语（冒号引出）：by listening to which theme ... they may be able to monitor where ... and how ...（介词 + which 宾语从句；where/how 宾语从句并列）
考点：because 原因状语从句；offer sb sth 双宾语；"介词 + which" 宾语从句；where/how 宾语从句并列
译文：由于唱歌的鲸在水下极难观察，这一发现为研究者提供了一扇"声学窗口"：通过听一头鲸正在唱哪个主题，他们或许就能监测它在潜水中的位置与身体的运动方式，而无须真正看到它。
Humpback song is culturally transmitted — learned, modified and shared within populations — and its intricate structure is now shown to be inseparable from the physical act of diving, a feat that demands careful management of air and energy under crushing pressure.
主干：Humpback song is culturally transmitted ... and its intricate structure is now shown to be inseparable from the physical act of diving（并列句）
插入语（过去分词并列）：learned, modified and shared within populations（补充说明"文化传播"）
同位语 + 定语从句：a feat that demands careful management of air and energy（解释 diving；that 定语从句）
考点：并列句；过去分词并列作插入语；be inseparable from；同位语；that 定语从句
译文：座头鲸的歌声是文化传播的——在种群内部习得、修改与共享——而其精细复杂的结构如今被证明与潜水的身体动作密不可分，这一壮举要求在巨大水压下精心调配空气与能量。

四、全文大意

座头鲸的歌声是动物界最复杂的声音之一，雄性会在繁殖季反复吟唱由呻吟、鸣叫与低吟构成的旋律，动辄连续数小时，但海洋生物学家始终困惑：这头唱歌的鲸在水下到底在做什么。雪城大学的一支团队在毛伊岛附近展开研究，把能同时记录声音与运动的吸盘式标签装到 16 头成年鲸身上，抓取了数千次潜水及其伴随的歌声。结果发表在《当代生物学》上，揭示出鲸歌结构与潜水阶段之间惊人而紧密的对应关系：特定的乐句主题与潜水循环的特定阶段相匹配，仿佛身体每个动作都对应某段特定旋律。具体而言，鲸下潜时唱一套主题，转身上升时唱另一套，而潜水的最深处恰与最繁复的乐段相伴——这说明鲸并非随机歌唱，而是围绕自身呼吸与运动的节奏编排曲目。这一发现的意义远不止猎奇：由于唱歌的鲸在水下极难观察，它为研究者提供了一扇"声学窗口"，通过"听"就能推断鲸在潜水中的位置与姿态。它同时也深化了我们对鲸为何歌唱的理解——座头鲸的歌声是文化传播的，在种群内习得、修改与共享，其精细结构与潜水的身体动作密不可分，而潜水需要在巨大水压下精心调配空气与能量。研究者形容"歌声并非浮在行为之上，而是被编织进行为之中"，并指出这种对应关系有助于在视觉追踪不可能的海域与深度远程监测鲸的生理状态。不过作者也谨慎说明局限：样本仅 16 头、来自单一繁殖地，这种"歌—潜"对应是否适用于其他种群、雌鲸或幼鲸，仍是待解之谜。对研究并热爱海洋的人而言，这项研究传递了更深的信息：即便是大海里最熟悉的声音，也仍藏着秘密，而理解这些秘密，或许是保护那些发出歌声的、行踪隐秘的动物所不可或缺的——它们的隐秘生活，我们才刚刚开始"听见"。
