当城市灯光成为"生态陷阱"：人造光如何扰乱候鸟的夜间迁徙

A radar-and-satellite study finds that artificial light is the strongest predictor of where migrating birds stop to rest — drawing them into bright but barren "ecological traps"
来源 Nature Communications / ScienceDaily / Audubon（综合）作者 外刊精读专栏日期 2026-09-15难度 难

中文导读

在人类历史的大部分时间里，夜空是一张可靠的"地图"——无数生灵借着恒星的方位指引自己的一生。然而这张地图正被一点点抹去：人造光从城市向外蔓延，把曾经漆黑的夜空染成发光的雾霭。一项发表在《自然·通讯》的研究，把美国上空的鸟类雷达观测与夜间灯光卫星数据结合起来，首次清晰回答了长期困扰科学家的一个问题——疲惫的候鸟落地过夜时，究竟靠什么选择落脚点？答案既清晰又令人不安：人造光是候鸟选择停歇地的"最强预测因子"，其影响力胜过温度、植被与水源。作者指出，这正是"生态陷阱"的典型特征：在自然环境中，动物用来判断一个地方的线索是可靠的指南，而人造光却让这些线索与现实脱钩，把候鸟诱入明亮却贫瘠、食物匮乏又危机四伏的城市。从"关灯计划"到向下照射的灯罩，光污染恰恰是最容易逆转的环境危害——黑暗本身，其实是一片需要我们去守护的栖息地。

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

【1】For most of human history, the night sky was a reliable map — a vast, predictable dome of stars by which countless creatures steered their lives. That map is now being erased. Artificial light, spreading outward from cities, has turned the once-dark horizon into a glowing haze, with the consequences for animals that navigate by starlight only beginning to be understood.
【2】The scale of the problem has been made newly visible by a study published in the journal Nature Communications, which combined radar observations of migrating birds with satellite measurements of nighttime light across the United States. Tracking birds through the air, the researchers could at last ask a question that had long eluded scientists: when a weary migrant touches down for the night, what determines where it lands?
【3】The answer was as clear as it was troubling. Artificial light, the team found, was the single strongest predictor of where migrating birds chose to stop — stronger than temperature, stronger than vegetation or water. Where a city's glow burned brightest, birds gathered in numbers that bore little relation to the quality of the habitat below.
【4】This, the authors argue, is the signature of an "ecological trap." In a natural landscape, the cues an animal uses to judge a place are reliable guides to its true value. Artificial light decouples those cues from reality, luring birds into habitats that harbor little food or shelter — bright, yet dangerously barren.
【5】The mechanism reaches back to the deep past. Many nocturnal migrants steer by celestial cues, reading the stars and the moon as a compass — an instinct they cannot easily unlearn. A sky veiled in artificial glow can disorient a bird mid-flight, drawing it off its trajectory and onto a costly detour toward the very cities that produced the light.
【6】The costs are concrete. Studies document migrants circling illuminated towers until exhaustion, colliding with glass by the hundreds of millions each year, and falling prey to predators that have learned to hunt beneath the glow. Over time, the disruption may shift the timing of migration itself, with effects that ripple through entire ecosystems.
【7】The researchers are careful to acknowledge the limits of their work. Light is rarely the only stressor a migrant faces, and the pathways from a bright horizon to a bird's death remain, in many cases, conjectural. Yet the association, they note, is hard to dismiss, arriving just as the world's dark places are in steep decline.
【8】The hopeful corollary is that light pollution is, in principle, the easiest of environmental harms to reverse. "Lights Out" campaigns, which ask cities to dim or extinguish unnecessary illumination during migration seasons, have already cut bird collisions, and shielded, downward-facing lighting can preserve human safety while restoring darkness overhead.
【9】For those of us who rarely look up at night, the finding is a quiet invitation. Darkness is not the absence of anything; it is a refuge in its own right, a habitat that billions of creatures depend on, and one we can begin to restore — not with a grand gesture, but by simply turning down the lights.

段落主旨

  1. 夜空这张"地图"正被人造光抹去，靠星光导航的动物受影响。
  2. 研究结合雷达与卫星数据，追问候鸟落地时选择哪里过夜。
  3. 人造光是候鸟选择停歇地的最强预测因子，胜过温度与植被。
  4. 这是"生态陷阱"：光把线索与现实脱钩，把鸟诱入亮却贫瘠之地。
  5. 机理：夜间候鸟靠天体导航，光遮蔽星空、使其偏航绕道。
  6. 代价：绕塔盘旋至力竭、撞玻璃、被捕食，迁徙时序受扰。
  7. 研究者承认局限：光非唯一压力，机理部分仍属推测，但关联难以忽视。
  8. 转机：光污染最易逆转，"关灯"计划已见效。
  9. 启示：黑暗本身是一种栖息地，关灯即可恢复。

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

The scale of the problem has been made newly visible by a study published in the journal Nature Communications, which combined radar observations of migrating birds with satellite measurements of nighttime light across the United States.
主干：The scale of the problem has been made newly visible by a study（主语 + has been made + 宾补 + by 施动者，被动语态）
过去分词作后置定语：published in the journal Nature Communications（修饰 study）
非限制性定语从句：which combined radar observations of migrating birds with satellite measurements of nighttime light（which 指代 study；combined A with B 搭配；migrating birds 现在分词作定语）
考点：现在完成时被动语态；过去分词作后置定语；which 非限制性定语从句；combine A with B
译文：这个问题的规模，最近被发表在《自然·通讯》上的一项研究清晰地揭示出来——该研究把美国上空的候鸟雷达观测与夜间灯光卫星测量结合了起来。
Many nocturnal migrants steer by celestial cues, reading the stars and the moon as a compass — an instinct they cannot easily unlearn.
主干：Many nocturnal migrants steer by celestial cues（主语 + steer + 方式状语 by ...）
现在分词作伴随状语：reading the stars and the moon as a compass（reading 表伴随，read ... as ..."把……当作……来解读"）
同位语 + 定语从句：an instinct they cannot easily unlearn（an instinct 概括前面内容；they cannot easily unlearn 为省略关系代词的定语从句）
考点：现在分词作伴随状语；同位语；省略关系代词的定语从句
译文：许多夜间迁徙的候鸟靠天体线索导航，把星星和月亮当作指南针来解读——这是一种它们难以轻易舍弃的本能。
A sky veiled in artificial glow can disorient a bird mid-flight, drawing it off its trajectory and onto a costly detour toward the very cities that produced the light.
主干：A sky ... can disorient a bird mid-flight（主语 + can disorient + 宾语 + 时间状语 mid-flight）
过去分词作后置定语：veiled in artificial glow（修饰 sky，"被人造光遮蔽的天空"）
现在分词作结果状语：drawing it off its trajectory and onto a costly detour（drawing 表结果；off ... and onto ... 并列介词短语）
定语从句：that produced the light（修饰 cities）
考点：过去分词作后置定语；现在分词作结果状语；that 定语从句；the very + 名词 表强调
译文：被人造光遮蔽的天空，能让一只飞行中的鸟迷失方向，把它拽离原来的航线，转而绕道飞向那些恰恰制造了这些光的城市。
Studies document migrants circling illuminated towers until exhaustion, colliding with glass by the hundreds of millions each year, and falling prey to predators that have learned to hunt beneath the glow.
主干：Studies document migrants（主语 + document + 宾语）
三个并列现在分词作宾语补足语：circling illuminated towers until exhaustion / colliding with glass ... / falling prey to predators ...（描述 migrants 的三种状态）
过去分词作定语：illuminated towers（"被照亮的塔楼"）
定语从句：that have learned to hunt beneath the glow（修饰 predators）
考点：现在分词作宾语补足语；过去分词作定语；that 定语从句；by the hundreds of millions 表数量
译文：研究记录了候鸟围着被照亮的塔楼盘旋直至力竭、每年数以亿计地撞上玻璃、以及沦为那些学会在光晕下捕食的天敌之猎物等种种情形。
Darkness is not the absence of anything; it is a refuge in its own right, a habitat that billions of creatures depend on, and one we can begin to restore — not with a grand gesture, but by simply turning down the lights.
主干（并列句）：Darkness is not the absence of anything; it is a refuge ...（分号连接两个并列分句）
表语 1：not the absence of anything（"不是什么都没有"）
表语 2 + 同位语：a refuge ... a habitat that billions of creatures depend on, and one we can begin to restore（a habitat 与 one 为 refuge 的同位语）
定语从句：that billions of creatures depend on（修饰 habitat；depend on 的 on 提前）；we can begin to restore（省略关系代词，修饰 one）
对比结构：not with a grand gesture, but by simply turning down the lights（not ... but ..."不是……而是……"）
考点：分号并列；同位语；"介词 + which/that" 定语从句；not ... but ... 结构；动名词短语 turning down
译文：黑暗并非"什么都没有"；它本身就是一片庇护所，是数十亿生灵赖以生存的栖息地，也是一片我们能够开始恢复的地方——不必靠什么壮举，只需把灯调暗一点。

四、全文大意

在人类历史的大部分时间里，夜空是一张可靠的"地图"——一个广阔而可预测的星空穹顶，无数生灵借它指引自己的一生。然而这张地图正在被抹去：人造光从城市向外蔓延，把曾经漆黑的夜空变成发光的雾霭，而它对靠星光导航的动物带来的后果，才刚刚开始被认识。发表在《自然·通讯》上的一项研究，把美国上空的候鸟雷达观测与夜间灯光卫星测量结合起来，首次清晰地回答了长期困扰科学家的问题：疲惫的候鸟落地过夜时，究竟靠什么选择落脚点？答案既清晰又令人不安——人造光是候鸟选择停歇地的"最强预测因子"，其影响力胜过温度、胜过植被或水源；在城市灯光最亮的地方，聚集的鸟群数量与下方栖息地的质量几乎毫无关系。作者指出，这正是"生态陷阱"的典型特征：在自然环境中，动物用来判断一个地方的线索——亮度、温暖、表面的丰饶——是判断其真实价值的可靠指南；而人造光却让这些线索与现实脱钩，把候鸟诱入明亮却贫瘠、食物匮乏又危机四伏的地方。这一机理可以追溯到远古：许多夜间迁徙的候鸟靠天体线索导航，把星星和月亮当作难以舍弃的指南针；被人造光遮蔽的天空会让飞行中的鸟迷失方向，把它拽离航线、绕道飞向制造这些光的城市。代价是实实在在的：研究记录了候鸟围着被照亮的塔楼盘旋直至力竭、每年数以亿计地撞上玻璃、以及沦为学会在光晕下捕食的天敌之猎物；久而久之，这种干扰甚至可能改变迁徙的时序，其影响波及整个生态系统。研究者谨慎地承认研究的局限：光很少是候鸟面临的唯一压力，从明亮的夜空到一只鸟的死亡之间，许多环节仍属推测；但他们也指出，这种关联的强度难以忽视，而它恰恰出现在世界上黑暗之地急剧减少的时刻。值得欣慰的是，光污染在原理上是最容易逆转的环境危害——"关灯"计划呼吁城市在迁徙季节调暗或熄灭不必要的照明，已显著减少了鸟类撞击，而加装灯罩、向下照射的灯具，既能保障人类安全，又能恢复头顶的黑暗。对我们这些很少在夜里抬头的人来说，这项发现是一份安静的邀请：黑暗并非"什么都没有"，它本身就是一片庇护所，是数十亿生灵赖以生存的栖息地，也是一片我们能够开始恢复的地方——不必靠什么壮举，只需把灯调暗一点。
