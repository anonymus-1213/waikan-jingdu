瑞安航空警告：油价若居高不下，欧洲机票价格明年将大幅上涨

Ryanair warns air fares in Europe will jump next year if oil price stays high
来源 The Guardian 卫报作者 卫报商务版日期 2026-09-02难度 中等

一、原文精读

鼠标悬停在带 蓝色虚线下划线 的生词上，即可查看词性和释义。

1Ryanair has warned that air fares in Europe will jump next year if the oil price remains high and that some airlines could go bust.
2The Irish budget carrier has cut its passenger target for the year to 31 March, down from 216 million customers to 214 million, to help reduce its exposure to "unhedged winter oil" during the unprofitable off season, with jet fuel currently trading at $140 (£104) a barrel.
3The company said: "If high oil prices continue through to summer 2027, Ryanair believes short-haul air fares in Europe will increase materially to reflect higher oil prices, as some less well-hedged competitors will struggle to maintain capacity or even survive this coming winter season."
4Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, touched $97.04 a barrel on Wednesday, the highest since late July, after renewed clashes between the US and Iran fuelled concerns over supply. It later eased back to just below $95.
5Ryanair expects the cut to its winter schedule to reduce its winter losses by €70m (£60m) to €100m. But, because it has hedged 80% of its jet fuel at $67 a barrel, it expects to record another profitable year, albeit below last year's record profit after tax.
6The airline said it was on track to increase summer passenger numbers – between April and October – by more than 5% from 138 million to 145 million.
7The rival budget airline Wizz Air reported on Wednesday that its passenger numbers had grown by 25.9% last month compared with a year earlier, driven by a jump in flight capacity.

三、长难句分析（三步拆解法）

"If high oil prices continue through to summer 2027, Ryanair believes short-haul air fares in Europe will increase materially to reflect higher oil prices, as some less well-hedged competitors will struggle to maintain capacity or even survive this coming winter season."
条件状语从句：If high oil prices continue through to summer 2027（如果高油价持续到 2027 年夏季）
主干：Ryanair believes（主句，后面为宾语从句）
宾语从句：short-haul air fares in Europe will increase materially（欧洲短途票价将大幅上涨）
目的状语：to reflect higher oil prices（以反映更高的油价）
原因状语从句：as some less well-hedged competitors will struggle to maintain capacity or even survive（因为一些对冲不足的竞争对手将难以维持运力，甚至难以撑过这个冬天）
译文：瑞安航空表示："如果高油价持续到 2027 年夏季，瑞安认为欧洲的短途机票价格将大幅上涨以反映更高的油价，因为一些对冲不足的竞争对手将难以维持运力，甚至难以撑过这个冬季航季。"
The Irish budget carrier has cut its passenger target for the year to 31 March, down from 216 million customers to 214 million, to help reduce its exposure to "unhedged winter oil" during the unprofitable off season, with jet fuel currently trading at $140 (£104) a barrel.
主干：The Irish budget carrier has cut its passenger target（这家爱尔兰廉价航空已下调其乘客目标）
插入语：down from 216 million customers to 214 million（从 2.16 亿降至 2.14 亿）
目的状语：to help reduce its exposure to "unhedged winter oil"（以降低其对"未对冲冬季燃油"的风险敞口）
时间状语：during the unprofitable off season（在无利可图的淡季）
独立主格结构：with jet fuel currently trading at $140 (£104) a barrel（with + 名词 + 现在分词，表伴随，航空燃油现价每桶 140 美元）
译文：这家爱尔兰廉价航空已将其截至 3 月 31 日年度的乘客目标从 2.16 亿人次下调至 2.14 亿人次，以降低其在无利可图的淡季对"未对冲冬季燃油"的风险敞口——目前航空燃油交易价为每桶 140 美元（104 英镑）。
But, because it has hedged 80% of its jet fuel at $67 a barrel, it expects to record another profitable year, albeit below last year's record profit after tax.
主干：it expects to record another profitable year（它预计将再度录得盈利年度）
原因状语从句：because it has hedged 80% of its jet fuel at $67 a barrel（因为它已以每桶 67 美元对冲了 80% 的航空燃油）
让步状语（省略）：albeit below last year's record profit after tax（尽管低于去年创纪录的税后利润）
译文：但由于它已按每桶 67 美元对冲了 80% 的航空燃油，它预计将再度录得一个盈利年度，尽管将低于去年创纪录的税后利润。
Brent crude, the global oil benchmark, touched $97.04 a barrel on Wednesday, the highest since late July, after renewed clashes between the US and Iran fuelled concerns over supply.
主干：Brent crude touched $97.04 a barrel on Wednesday（布伦特原油周三触及每桶 97.04 美元）
同位语：the global oil benchmark（全球石油基准）
同位语（补充）：the highest since late July（自 7 月下旬以来的最高水平）
原因状语从句：after renewed clashes between the US and Iran fuelled concerns over supply（在美伊冲突再起加剧供应担忧之后）
译文：全球石油基准布伦特原油周三触及每桶 97.04 美元，为 7 月下旬以来最高水平，此前美伊冲突再起加剧了市场对供应的担忧。

四、全文大意

爱尔兰廉价航空瑞安航空警告，若油价持续高企，欧洲机票价格明年将大幅上涨，部分对冲不足的航空公司甚至可能破产。为此，瑞安将截至 3 月 31 日年度的乘客目标从 2.16 亿下调至 2.14 亿，以减少对"未对冲冬季燃油"的风险敞口，预计冬季亏损可削减 7000 万至 1 亿欧元。目前航空燃油交易价约为每桶 140 美元，布伦特原油周三触及 97.04 美元这一 7 月以来高位。不过，由于瑞安已按每桶 67 美元对冲了 80% 的燃油，公司预计仍能保持盈利，只是低于去年的纪录水平。与此同时，竞争对手 Wizz Air 上月客运量同比增长 25.9%，显示欧洲短途航空市场仍在扩张。

五、思考与仿写

思考题：为什么瑞安航空在油价高企时仍能保持盈利？结合第 5 段，谈谈"燃油对冲（fuel hedging）"对企业应对能源价格波动的意义，并思考高油价对消费者与航空业整体会带来哪些连锁影响。
仿写题：仿照第 3 段的句式 "If … continue, … believes … will … , as … will struggle to …"，围绕「原材料价格上涨」或「房租上涨」写一个句子。
